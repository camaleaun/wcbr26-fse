WCBR2026 pairs two typefaces: **Poppins** for headings and labels, **Montserrat** for body
copy. Type is set on an **18px base** with a sharp, editorial feel — headings are heavy
(weight 900), uppercase, and colored with the primary cyan.

## Typefaces

| Role | Family | Weights loaded | Sass token |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headings & labels | **Poppins** | 400, 600, 700, 900 | `$headings-font-family` |
| Body | **Montserrat** | 400, 700 | `$font-family-sans-serif` |

Both are self-hosted as `woff2` with `font-display: swap`, falling back to
`system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`.

## Base

```scss
$font-family-sans-serif: "Montserrat", system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif;
$headings-font-family:   "Poppins", system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif;
$headings-font-weight:   900;
$headings-color:         $primary;   // $cyan-700 · #00595d
$font-size-base:         1.125rem;   // 18px
$line-height-base:       1.45;
```

## Headings

Headings use Poppins **900**, `text-transform: uppercase`, primary color, and a tight
`line-height` (~1.1). Sizes are fluid (`clamp`) so they scale with the viewport.

| Level | Size (`clamp`) | Letter-spacing |
|---|---|---|
| `h1` / `.h1` | `clamp(2.25rem, 6.2vw, 3.625rem)` | `-0.02em` |
| `h2` / `.h2` | `clamp(1.9rem, 5vw, 3rem)` | `-0.01em` |
| `h3` / `.h3` | inherited ramp | — |

<div class="bd-example">
  <h1 class="text-uppercase" style="color:var(--wcbr-primary);font-weight:900;line-height:1.1">WordCamp Brasil 2026</h1>
  <h2 class="text-uppercase" style="color:var(--wcbr-primary);font-weight:900;line-height:1.1">What to expect</h2>
  <h3 class="text-uppercase" style="color:var(--wcbr-primary);font-weight:900;line-height:1.1">Latest news</h3>
</div>

## Body & lead

Body text is Montserrat at the 18px base with `line-height: 1.45`. The intro/lead paragraph
scales up fluidly.

```html
<p class="lead">The largest WordPress conference in Latin America, in Belo Horizonte.</p>
```

<div class="bd-example">
  <p class="lead">The largest WordPress conference in Latin America, in Belo Horizonte.</p>
  <p>Talks, lightning talks, panels and lots of networking with the community.</p>
</div>

## Eyebrows & labels

A recurring pattern across the site: small, **uppercase** labels in Poppins with positive
letter-spacing — used for section eyebrows, stat labels, tags, and nav items. Colors vary by
context (`--wcbr-orange-600` for tags, `--wcbr-cyan-800` for nav, `--wcbr-gray-700` for meta).

| Use | Weight | Size | Letter-spacing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Section eyebrow | 600 | ~0.81rem | `.06em` |
| Stat label | 400 | ~0.95rem | — |
| Tag | 700 | ~0.625rem | `.06em` |
| Nav item | 700 | ~0.8rem | `.02em` |

<div class="bd-example">
  <span class="text-uppercase d-block" style="font-weight:700;font-size:.625rem;letter-spacing:.06em;color:var(--wcbr-orange-600)">Call for Speakers</span>
  <span class="text-uppercase d-block" style="font-weight:600;font-size:.8125rem;letter-spacing:.06em;color:var(--wcbr-orange-600)">About the event</span>
</div>

## Notes

- Headings default to uppercase in the WCBR brand — do not rely on sentence case for `h1`–`h3`.
- Because `$border-radius: 0`, type sits on hard-edged surfaces; keep labels tight and aligned.
- These values are taken from the `html-from-figma` prototype (`assets/css/styles.css`) and the
  theme's `scss/_variables.scss`.
