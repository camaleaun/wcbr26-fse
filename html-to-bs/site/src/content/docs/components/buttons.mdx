Square corners, heavy uppercase Poppins, a 2px border, and a **hard offset shadow** that
presses in on click. Both variants share this treatment: `.btn-primary` and `.btn-orange`.

## Variants

| Class | Fill | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `.btn-primary` | `$cyan-700` `#00595d` | Standard primary action |
| `.btn-orange` | `$orange-600` `#c6360b` | The event CTA, tickets and newsletter |

There is **one** orange button, not several. See [Contrast](#contrast) for why.

<div class="wcbrd-example d-flex flex-wrap align-items-center gap-4">
  <a class="btn btn-primary" href="#">View schedule</a>
  <a class="btn btn-orange" href="#">Get your ticket</a>
</div>

Sizes are a separate concern. See [Sizing](#sizing) below.

## Contrast

The orange scale needs the right text color per shade. Pick whichever passes:

| Token | Hex | vs. white | vs. black |
|---|---|---|---|
| `$orange-500` | `#f56530` | 3.09:1, fails WCAG AA | ~6.7:1, passes ✓ |
| `$orange-600` | `#c6360b` | 5.34:1, passes ✓ | fails |

`.btn-orange` fills with `$orange-600` and white text. `$orange-500` is lighter. Pair it with
black text if it's ever used as a fill. Otherwise it stays an accent for borders and sticker
shadows.

## The sticker shadow

The offset shadow reads its color from the variant's own border. Change the border, the shadow
follows. No per-button CSS needed.

Both variants are two-tone. The fill stays one shade. The border, and the shadow, sits a step
darker or lighter: `$cyan-900` under the `$cyan-700` fill for primary, `$orange-500` under the
`$orange-600` fill for orange.

## Interaction

| State | Shadow | Transform |
|---|---|---|
| Rest | `5px 5px 0` | none |
| Hover | `3px 3px 0` | `translate(2px, 2px)` |
| Active | `0 0 0` | `translate(5px, 5px)` |
| Disabled | none | none |

## Sizing

There are two sizes. Base `.btn` is the small one, used only by the navbar "Ingressos" CTA.
`.btn-lg` is the big sticker CTA: hero, sponsor, newsletter, drawer, everywhere else.

<div class="wcbrd-example d-flex flex-wrap align-items-center gap-4">
  <a class="btn btn-orange fw-black" href="#">Tickets</a>
  <a class="btn btn-orange btn-lg" href="#">Get your ticket</a>
</div>

`.btn-lg` carries its own weight (900) automatically. The base size needs `.fw-black` added
when it should look just as heavy.

The sponsor CTA ("Torne-se um patrocinador") is one deliberate change from the prototype. It's
primary cyan, not ticket orange. That keeps it visually apart from the ticket and newsletter
CTAs. See [Variants](#variants).

## Notes

- Do not round the corners. Square corners are intentional and brand-defining.
- Labels are uppercase Poppins 900. Keep them short and imperative.
- `.btn-primary` is **cyan**, not orange. Use `.btn-orange` for the ticket CTA.
