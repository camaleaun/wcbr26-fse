WCBR has two label patterns. A **filled badge** for standout metadata, like the hero. A
**text-only tag** for card and header eyebrows.

## Badge

`.badge` paired with a `.text-bg-*` color. The brand treatment lives on the `.badge` base:
Poppins, uppercase, letter-spacing, 2px radius. Every color variant inherits it.

<div class="wcbrd-example d-flex flex-wrap align-items-center gap-3">
  <span class="badge text-bg-primary">Belo Horizonte</span>
  <span class="badge text-bg-orange">October 2026</span>
  <span class="badge text-bg-dark">October 30–31</span>
</div>

<div class="wcbrd-callout wcbrd-callout-warning">

**There is no `.badge-orange` or `.badge-primary`.** `.text-bg-orange` sets the background
**and** picks a readable foreground in one class. Reach for `.text-bg-orange`, not
`.badge-orange`.

</div>

### Sizing

Badge geometry matches the hero chip in the design: `.9rem` label, weight 600, `9px`/`16px`
padding, `2px` corner radius.

## Tag

A **tag** is a small, uppercase, letter-spaced label with **no fill**. Used above card titles
and in the header. Montserrat 600, `$orange-900`, `.8125rem`, `letter-spacing: .06em`.

<div class="wcbrd-example">
  <span class="tag">Call for Speakers</span>
</div>

Tags carry meaning through color and case, not a fill. Do not give `.tag` a background.

## Notes

- Badge fills must clear 4.5:1 against white label text. `$orange-500` (3.09:1 vs. white) isn't
  used as a badge fill. `$orange-600` (5.34:1) is. See [Contrast](/docs/2026/components/buttons#contrast).
- The old `.hero-chip` class is gone. It is a plain `.badge` now.
