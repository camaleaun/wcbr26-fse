The site header has a logo, a horizontal menu with dropdowns, event meta (social, hashtags,
date), and the *Tickets* CTA. Below `xl` the menu collapses into an offcanvas drawer.

## Structure

The header is a sticky bar with a bottom border. Inside it: the brand logo, a horizontal nav
list (visible `xl` and up) with two dropdown items ("Event" and its submenu), the *Tickets*
CTA, and a menu toggler that only shows below `xl`.

## Dropdowns

Desktop submenus open on click, replacing the prototype's hover-based submenus — the caret
icon is drawn by the toggle itself, so the prototype's hand-rolled caret SVG is dropped.

Nav links are Poppins 700 uppercase in `$cyan-700`, with a 3px transparent bottom border that
turns orange on hover.

## Offcanvas drawer

On mobile the whole menu becomes an offcanvas panel opening from the end side. The header's
"Event" submenu becomes a collapsible sub-list inside the drawer, toggled by its own button.
The drawer's caret rotates 180° when its control is expanded, driven purely by
`[aria-expanded="true"]` — no bespoke JavaScript.

## Notes

- All the custom drawer JavaScript is replaced by declarative data attributes — the site ships
  no bespoke nav script.
- Keep `aria-label` on the brand link and the toggler; the logo image carries the alt text.
- The header sticks to the top (`.sticky-top`) with a bottom border, over `$sand-100`.
