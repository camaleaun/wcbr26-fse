The Jio World Convention Centre in Mumbai is designed with accessibility as a core feature rather than an afterthought. The venue offers a fully wheelchair-friendly layout, with step-free access across entrances, exits, and internal pathways. Wide corridors, spacious halls, and thoughtfully planned circulation areas make it easy for individuals using mobility aids to navigate without unnecessary obstacles or detours.

Movement within the venue is supported by multiple elevators and barrier-free pathways that connect different levels seamlessly. Given the scale of the convention centre, this vertical and horizontal accessibility plays a critical role in ensuring that visitors can move comfortably between halls, event zones, and common areas without fatigue or confusion. JWCC has the world’s largest passenger elevator with a capacity to carry more than 200 people.

### Accessible Restrooms and Facilities

Restrooms across the venue are designed to accommodate wheelchair users, with sufficient space and support features. Common areas, seating zones, and event spaces are also planned to ensure ease of access rather than creating bottlenecks.

### Assisted Mobility Services

For visitors who need additional support, wheelchairs and internal transport options such as golf carts are available on request. These can typically be arranged in advance or accessed on-site, making the experience more manageable for elderly guests or those with limited mobility.

### ******Clear signage and wayfinding******

Directional signage is placed at logical points across the venue, helping attendees orient themselves quickly. For those with visual or cognitive disabilities, the clean layouts and structured pathways reduce confusion and support smoother movement.

### Inclusive Dining and Event Areas

Food courts and dining spaces are structured with accessibility in mind. Layouts allow easy navigation, and seating arrangements are designed so that visitors using mobility aids can comfortably access and use these spaces.

### Parking and Drop-off Convenience

The venue provides accessible parking options and well-planned drop-off points close to entrances. Clear signage and organized traffic flow reduce the need for long walking distances, which is a practical win for visitors who require assistance.

</div>

<div class="wp-block-group has-global-padding is-layout-constrained wp-block-group-is-layout-constrained">

### On-ground Support and Assistance

Staff and help desks are available throughout the venue to assist visitors when needed. This adds a human layer to accessibility, ensuring that support is not limited to infrastructure alone.

### Practical Consideration

While the infrastructure is strong, certain advanced assistive devices such as powered wheelchairs may not always be available on-site. Visitors who rely on specific equipment should plan accordingly.
