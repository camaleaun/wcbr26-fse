### **What Are WordCamp Side Events?**

WordCamp Side Events are **independent, community-led activities** that happen alongside the main WordCamp conference. They are not part of the official schedule. Instead, they are organised by community members, sponsors, companies, and local volunteers who want to create extra value for attendees.

Think of them as the after-hours brain fuel of WordCamp.

Since the very first **WordCamp Asia in 2023 in Bangkok**, side events have become a powerful tradition. They have sparked collaborations, created friendships across countries, and opened doors to real business opportunities. We are continuing that momentum at WordCamp Asia 2026.

If you believe community grows through conversation and connection, this is your moment.

### **Important Guidelines for Organising WordCamp Asia Side Events**

Before you submit your proposal, please review the following expectations carefully. These guidelines protect attendees, organisers, and the larger WordPress community.

#### **1. Accountability**

The side event organiser is fully responsible for:

- Event content and format
- Venue arrangements and logistics
- Attendee safety and overall experience
- Compliance with the WordPress Code of Conduct

We strongly recommend reviewing the [**Contributor Covenant**](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) to ensure your event aligns with inclusive and respectful community standards. WordCamp Asia is built on trust and shared values. Your event should reflect the same level of professionalism and responsibility.

#### **2. No Conflict with the Official Schedule**

Side events must not overlap with official WordCamp Asia 2026 sessions.

The goal is to complement the conference, not compete with it. Please review the published schedule before finalising your timing. Strategic planning ensures maximum participation and a better experience for everyone.

### **How To Submit A Side Event For WordCamp Asia?**

We are excited to hear that you are planning an event around **WordCamp Asia 2026**. If you would like your event to be featured on the official WCAsia 2026 Side Events page, please complete the form below with all required details.

Our team will review your submission before publishing it.

### **Important Notes**

- Organisers and attendees take full responsibility for organising and participating in side events. 
- Events that conflict with the official WordCamp Asia schedule will not be listed.
- Please clearly mention your event date and time in the submission form.

We aim to maintain clarity, transparency, and a seamless experience for all attendees.

### **How to Submit Your Side Event**

#### **1. Plan Your Event**

Confirm the following before submitting:

- Venue or location
- Date and time
- Capacity and registration requirements
- Any entry criteria or ticket details

#### **2. Complete the Registration Form**

Fill out the form below and provide accurate, detailed information about your event. Incomplete submissions may delay the review process.

#### **3. Await Review**

Once submitted, your event will be reviewed by the team. You will be notified about the status of your application. Approved events will be published on the WCAsia 2026 Side Events page.

***Please Note**: The organiser has full authority to manage event availability, approvals, and sign-ups. Please carefully review the organiser’s instructions to confirm your participation or secure your spot.*

All individuals who plan, host, or participate in WordCamp Asia Side Events do so at their own responsibility. By engaging in a side event, you acknowledge and accept full accountability for your involvement. 

If your goal is to promote products or services, we strongly encourage you to consider becoming an official [sponsor of WordCamp Asia](https://asia.wordcamp.org/2026/call-for-sponsors/) instead.
