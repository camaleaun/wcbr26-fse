Power the Future of Open Source at WordCamp Asia 2026

WordCamp Asia 2026 is coming to Jio World Convention Centre (JWCC), Mumbai, India, April 9–11, 2026, and we’re inviting sponsors to participate.

With 3000+ attendees expected, this three-day event will bring together the global WordPress community in a city known for its energy, creativity, and constant motion. Sponsoring WordCamp Asia 2026 places your brand at the heart of conversations with developers, designers, entrepreneurs, agencies, and enterprises from across Asia and beyond.

Your support goes beyond visibility—it helps keep the event affordable, inclusive, and truly community-driven.

Why Sponsor WordCamp Asia 2026?

WordCamp Asia is more than a tech conference; it’s a celebration of community, innovation, and collaboration. Sponsoring gives you the chance to make the event accessible, affordable, and inclusive, while also putting your brand at the heart of the action.

Here’s what’s in store for our sponsors this year:

Unparalleled Exposure

Showcase your brand to 3000+ attendees from across Asia and beyond, including developers, designers, agencies, entrepreneurs, and business leaders.

Meaningful Connections

Network with top talent, product makers, and community leaders, opening doors to partnerships and future collaborations.

Brand Credibility

Enjoy prominent placement across event signage, the official website, promotional emails, and social media campaigns, reaching a global audience.

Market Insights

Stay ahead of the curve by discovering the latest innovations, challenges, and trends shaping the WordPress ecosystem.

Celebrate Open Source

Join the event and give back to the platform powering over 43% of the web, while showcasing your company’s values.

Business Impact

Showcase your products/services on-site, generate qualified leads, and create lasting partnerships.
