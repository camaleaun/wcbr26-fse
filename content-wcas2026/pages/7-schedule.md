## Contributor Day

WordCamp Asia 2026 kicks off with Contributor Day on April 9, 2026 at the Jio World Convention Centre in Mumbai. Join thousands of WordPress contributors, whether you code or contribute in other ways, for a full day of supporting and growing the project. Check out <a href="https://asia.wordcamp.org/2026/contributor-day/make-teams/" data-type="link" data-id="https://asia.wordcamp.org/2026/contributor-day/make-teams/">Make Teams</a> for more information.

You can also join hands-on workshops, visit the Open-Source Library for real community insights, understand the release process with a Q&A panel, and check out <a href="https://asia.wordcamp.org/2026/youthcamp-is-back-at-wordcamp-asia-2026/" data-type="link" data-id="https://asia.wordcamp.org/2026/youthcamp-is-back-at-wordcamp-asia-2026/">YouthCamp</a> for younger participants to learn and build with guided support.\
\
Read more about Contributor Day [here](https://asia.wordcamp.org/2026/contributor-day/).
