**Introduction**

Have you ever sketched a feature that made WordPress interfaces more intuitive and beautiful? That’s the essence of the Design Team to craft visual experiences that power WordPress on a global scale! The Design Team shapes the user interface for WordPress Core, block editor, themes, and patterns, ensuring accessibility, consistency, and delight for millions of users and creators.

**What Does the Design Team Do?**\
The Design Team develops guidelines, prototypes, and assets for Core UI, default themes, and design tools like the block library.\
As a new contributor, you can start by:

- Providing feedback on Figma prototypes or design tickets in the [\#design](http://wordpress.slack.com/messages/design/) Slack channel. Every suggestion refines the user experience.
- Creating icons, patterns, or illustrations for upcoming features.
- Testing designs for accessibility and responsiveness across devices.

The team’s work makes WordPress visually cohesive and user-friendly worldwide.

**Your Path as a Contributor\**
\
No formal design degree is needed – enthusiasm for pixels and people is key. Grow step by step:

- Start small. Comment on open design issues, tweak color variables, or document patterns.
- Build proficiency. Prototype blocks, iterate on theme designs, or collaborate on release visuals.
- Lead creatively. Champion projects, mentor juniors, or define design system guidelines.

Every pixel you place elevates the WordPress aesthetics.

**Overcoming Challenges Together**

Design involves balancing innovation with Core constraints, accessibility standards, and multi-team input – collaboration unlocks solutions. It’s okay to iterate wildly; share early mocks for collective refinement.\
\
Team members support via Slack and office hours – seek feedback on tools like Figma or design tokens anytime.\
\
Even spotting usability gaps or suggesting references sparks meaningful progress.

**Why Join the Design Team?**

- Influence millions. Your designs appear in every WordPress site and editor.
- Master design systems. Hone skills in UI/UX, accessibility, and prototyping tools.
- Thrive in creativity. Connect with visual thinkers shaping WordPress’ look and feel.

Contributing to the Design Team is one of the most visually rewarding ways to give back. Every prototype, icon, and guideline beautifies WordPress.
