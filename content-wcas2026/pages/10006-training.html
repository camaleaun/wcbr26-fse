Introduction

Have you ever created a lesson that unlocked WordPress for a complete beginner? That’s the heart of the Training Team – crafting educational content that empowers users worldwide! The Training Team stewards Learn.WordPress.org, developing courses, workshops, lesson plans, and pathways to help people master using, extending, and contributing to WordPress.

What Does the Training Team Do?

The Training Team builds and maintains structured learning experiences on Learn.WordPress.org, from beginner tutorials to advanced developer tracks.
As a new contributor, you can start by:

  • Reviewing lesson drafts for clarity and accuracy. Every suggestion improves a learner’s journey.
  • Testing courses or lesson plans in real scenarios and providing feedback.
  • Translating content to reach global audiences in multiple languages.

The team’s resources equip millions to build skills, launch sites, and join the community.

Your Path as a Contributor

No teaching degree required – anyone passionate about WordPress can contribute. Grow step by step:

  • Start small. Edit for grammar, add screenshots, or review code snippets in lesson plans.
  • Build impact. Create full lessons, design assessments, or coordinate content for learning pathways.
  • Lead education. Mentor creators, shape the Faculty Program, or guide team strategy.

Every edit you make educates the next generation of WordPressers.

How to prepare

To make the most of your Contributor Day experience, we recommend reviewing the team’s call for contributors. It includes details on what to expect, how to get started, and any prerequisites.

Overcoming Challenges Together

Creating quality content means balancing accuracy, engagement, and accessibility. The team works collaboratively to refine ideas and raise the standard together.

Iteration is part of the process. Focus on clear learning objectives and listen closely to learner feedback. That is where real improvement happens.

Even identifying gaps in topics or suggesting practical enhancements can drive meaningful change. Insight is contribution.

Why Join the Training Team?

  • Transform lives. Your content helps users gain skills and confidence.
  • Deepen your WordPress knowledge while teaching others.
  • Shape education. Join a collaborative group dedicated to accessible, high-quality learning.
