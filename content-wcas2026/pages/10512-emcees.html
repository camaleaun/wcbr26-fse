As your guides throughout the event, our emcees will introduce speakers, highlight important announcements, and keep the audience engaged between sessions. Their presence helps maintain the rhythm of the event while creating a welcoming and vibrant atmosphere for everyone attending.

Get to know the voices behind the microphone and join us in celebrating the people who help bring the WordCamp Asia 2026 stage to life!
