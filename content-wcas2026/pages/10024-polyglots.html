Introduction

Ever opened WordPress and found it speaking your language like it was born there? That quiet magic comes from the Polyglots Team.

With more than 15,000 volunteers, they make WordPress available in 200 plus languages and locales, powering over half of all installations worldwide. This is scale with soul.

What Does the Polyglots Team Do?

The team translates WordPress Core, themes, plugins, documentation, and marketing content. They also localize for cultural relevance so WordPress does not just translate words, it respects context.

They ensure WordPress feels native everywhere, from right to left scripts to regional date formats and terminology standards.

The primary translation platform used is GlotPress, where contributors collaborate on strings across projects.

Your Path as a Contributor

No formal translation degree required. Fluency and commitment are enough to begin. Grow step by step:

Start Small

  • Translate simple interface strings or plugin descriptions in GlotPress for your native language team. Every phrase moves WordPress closer to your community.

Build Expertise

  • Review translations for accuracy and consistency. Become a Translation Editor and validate contributions. Support your locale team.

Lead Globally

  • Grow into roles such as General Translation Editor or Locale Manager. Organize translation events. Guide new contributors.
  • Every string you translate connects WordPress to new users. That is direct impact.

Every string you translate connects WordPress to new audiences.

Overcoming Challenges Together

Translation involves nuances, technical terms, and evolving software – collaboration bridges gaps. It’s normal to debate word choices; focus on clarity and community input.

Team members support via locale-specific Slack channels and Rosetta sites – ask native speakers or PTEs anytime.

Even flagging ambiguous source strings or suggesting glossary terms advances quality.

Why Join the Polyglots Team?

  • Democratize access. Your translations empower non-English speakers to create confidently.
  • Preserve culture. Localize content to reflect regional voice and conventions.
  • Build global bonds. Connect with multilingual contributors united by WordPress.

Contributing to the Polyglots Team is one of the most inclusive ways to give back. Every translation breaks language barriers for WordPress.
