## **Brand Guidelines**

**Logo Usage:** Do not alter the aspect ratio, color, or orientation of the WordCamp Asia logo. Always maintain a clear space around the logo.

**Official Colors:**

**Primary color:**

- Full Blue – \#06116C
- Royal Violet – \#57066C

**Neutral**

- Dark Purple – \#2A047B
- Ivory White – \#FFFFF2
- Ghost White – \#F3F4F6
- Black (Body Text) – \#3C3C3C

**Accent Colors**

- Power Red – \#B52200
- Full Brown – \#652301
- Light Brown – \#EBA55F
- Spicy Red – \#66002A
- Primary: Hex \#06116C
- Accent: Hex \#57066C

**Typography:** Our official fonts are <a href="https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Jaini+Purva" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Jaini Purva</a> and <a href="https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Poppins" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Poppins</a>. If unavailable, please use Sans-Serif.

## **WordCamp Asia 2026 Overview**

WordCamp Asia 2026 is the fourth edition of the flagship WordCamp event in Asia. 

After successful editions in [Bangkok](https://asia.wordcamp.org/2023/), [Taipei](https://asia.wordcamp.org/2024/), and [Manila](https://asia.wordcamp.org/2025/), this year’s event continues the spirit of an open-source web summit and welcomes agencies, creators, students, and digital professionals from different backgrounds.

The event will be held in Mumbai, India’s financial capital and the home of Bollywood. With more than 3,000 attendees expected, WordCamp Asia 2026 aims to bring together technology, creativity, and culture in one place.

Organized by a dedicated team of volunteers, WordCamp Asia 2026 aims to be inclusive, innovative, and meaningful for the global open-source community.
