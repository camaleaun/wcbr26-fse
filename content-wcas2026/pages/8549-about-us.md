### WordPress Communities in the Host Country: India

<div class="wp-block-group has-roboto-font-family has-global-padding is-layout-constrained wp-block-group-is-layout-constrained">

India has one of the most active WordPress communities in the world, with thousands of contributors and more than [20](https://github.com/deshabhishek007/wp-india-communities) active community groups hosting regular meetups and events.

India’s WordCamp journey began with [WordCamp India 2009](https://india.wordcamp.org/2009/) in Delhi. Since then, the community has organized hundreds of events and has included next-gen new formats like Campus Connect and Women’s Day Meetups.

In 2025 alone, the country hosted over [15](https://central.wordcamp.org/schedule/past-wordcamps/) major events (WordCamps and next-gen events altogether), further showcasing its community strength.

Home to thousands of dedicated WordPress professionals, India is ready to host the premier WordPress conference of Asia — WordCamp Asia 2026.

Mumbai promises not just an amazing conference, but a magical experience. Known as India’s financial powerhouse and home to Bollywood, it combines iconic landmarks, a vibrant economy, and bustling local life, earning its nickname, the “City of Dreams.”

- **Iconic Landmarks & Coastline:** The ‘Queen’s Necklace’ (Marine Drive) and the historic Gateway of India offer stunning sea views and a glimpse into India’s culture and history.
- **Busy:** Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CST), a bustling train station, is famous for its historic architecture and scenes of everyday life. Mumbai is a city that never sleeps.
- **Street Food**: Try local favorites like vada pav, pav bhaji, and cutting chai from street vendors, simple treats loved by locals. Check our [food guide](https://asia.wordcamp.org/2026/mumbai-on-a-plate/) for more.
- **Markets**: Visit areas like Colaba Causeway or Crawford Market for shopping, souvenirs, and a slice of Mumbai life.
- **Haji Ali Dargah**: A peaceful mosque located on a small island, connected to land by a narrow path, especially nice for a walk when the tide is low.
- **Cultural Experiences**: Check out local art galleries, small theatres, and music performances to see Mumbai’s creative pulse. Check our [guide to Indian culture](https://asia.wordcamp.org/2026/namaste-india-guide-to-indian-culture/).

Mumbai mixes old and new, hustle and calm. Be sure to take some time to see the city, as it will give you a fuller experience alongside the conference. Check out more [things to do in Mumbai](https://asia.wordcamp.org/2026/things-to-do-in-mumbai/). Want to extend your stay?
