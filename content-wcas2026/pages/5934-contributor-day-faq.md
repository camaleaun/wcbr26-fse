### <span class="wp-block-accordion-heading__toggle-title">**Do I have to know how to code to contribute?**</span><span class="wp-block-accordion-heading__toggle-icon" aria-hidden="true">+</span>

<div id="accordion-item-40-panel" class="wp-block-accordion-panel is-layout-flow wp-block-accordion-panel-is-layout-flow" inert="" aria-labelledby="accordion-item-40" wp-bind--inert="!state.isOpen" role="region">

No. Coding is not required.

You can contribute through:

- Translations
- Testing
- Documentation
- Support
- Design
- Accessibility reviews
- Photography and media

Use the <a href="https://make.wordpress.org/contribute/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener nofollow">contributor wizard</a> before the event to identify teams that match your skills and interests.

Formerly known as the *Contributor Day – Human Library*, the Open-Source Library is a conversation-focused space.

You will meet **Open-Source Books** (people), experienced contributors who share real-world knowledge in specific areas of WordPress contribution.

You can:

- Choose a topic that interests you
- Check available time slots with the Head Librarian
- Have a one-on-one conversation
- Ask questions and learn from lived experience

### <span class="wp-block-accordion-heading__toggle-title">**I’ve never contributed before. Where should I start?**</span><span class="wp-block-accordion-heading__toggle-icon" aria-hidden="true">+</span>

<div id="accordion-item-44-panel" class="wp-block-accordion-panel is-layout-flow wp-block-accordion-panel-is-layout-flow" inert="" aria-labelledby="accordion-item-44" wp-bind--inert="!state.isOpen" role="region">

You are welcome here.

Contributor Day is designed for beginners as much as for experienced contributors.

Start by:

- Using the [Contributor Wizard](https://make.wordpress.org/contribute/)
- Attending beginner-friendly workshops
- Visiting the Open-Source Library for one-on-one conversations

No prior experience is needed. Just bring curiosity and a willingness to learn.

### <span class="wp-block-accordion-heading__toggle-title">**Which team should I join?**</span><span class="wp-block-accordion-heading__toggle-icon" aria-hidden="true">+</span>

<div id="accordion-item-45-panel" class="wp-block-accordion-panel is-layout-flow wp-block-accordion-panel-is-layout-flow" inert="" aria-labelledby="accordion-item-45" wp-bind--inert="!state.isOpen" role="region">

With **15+ Make WordPress teams**, there is a place for everyone, regardless of background or experience. Explore team overviews before the event on [make.wordpress.org](http://make.wordpress.org)

On the day:

The best team to join is the one that sparks your curiosity and makes you excited to contribute.

### <span class="wp-block-accordion-heading__toggle-title">**What accounts or tools do I need?**</span><span class="wp-block-accordion-heading__toggle-icon" aria-hidden="true">+</span>

<div id="accordion-item-46-panel" class="wp-block-accordion-panel is-layout-flow wp-block-accordion-panel-is-layout-flow" inert="" aria-labelledby="accordion-item-46" wp-bind--inert="!state.isOpen" role="region">

All contributors must have a **WordPress.org profile**. This is mandatory for participation and contribution credit. Your WordPress.org account also gives you access to the WordPress Slack workspace.

Some teams may use GitHub. Having a GitHub account is helpful but not mandatory for most non-development tasks.

**Recommended setup:**

- Laptop with a modern browser
- Code editor and local WordPress environment if contributing to development

### <span class="wp-block-accordion-heading__toggle-title">**I want to contribute to development teams. What should I do?**</span><span class="wp-block-accordion-heading__toggle-icon" aria-hidden="true">+</span>

<div id="accordion-item-47-panel" class="wp-block-accordion-panel is-layout-flow wp-block-accordion-panel-is-layout-flow" inert="" aria-labelledby="accordion-item-47" wp-bind--inert="!state.isOpen" role="region">

Set up your local development environment in advance.

Start with:

- The WordPress Development [Handbook](https://make.wordpress.org/core/handbook/)
- The official [GitHub getting started guide\
  ](https://github.com/WordPress/wordpress-develop?tab=readme-ov-file#getting-started)

On Contributor Day, visit the Core or Development team tables. Mentors will help you find beginner-friendly tasks and guide you through your first contribution.

### <span class="wp-block-accordion-heading__toggle-title">**Will there be mentors **that can help to guide me**?**</span><span class="wp-block-accordion-heading__toggle-icon" aria-hidden="true">+</span>

<div id="accordion-item-49-panel" class="wp-block-accordion-panel is-layout-flow wp-block-accordion-panel-is-layout-flow" inert="" aria-labelledby="accordion-item-49" wp-bind--inert="!state.isOpen" role="region">

Yes.

Table leads and experienced contributors will be available throughout the day to support you at every stage of your contribution. Whether you are brand new or already familiar with open source, they are there to help you succeed. They will help you:

- **Choose tasks**\
  Get guidance on selecting tasks that match your skills, interests, and experience level, including beginner-friendly issues or more advanced work.\
- **Understand workflows**\
  Learn how each team works, from tools and processes to where tasks are tracked and how contributions move from start to completion.\
- **Review contributions**\
  Receive feedback on your work before submission, including help with improving quality, following guidelines, and understanding next steps.\
- **Troubleshoot issues**\
  Get unstuck when you encounter technical problems, unclear instructions, or blockers during your contribution.

If you are experienced, they can also help you find advanced tasks and collaborate more effectively.

### <span class="wp-block-accordion-heading__toggle-title">**Do contributors get paid?**</span><span class="wp-block-accordion-heading__toggle-icon" aria-hidden="true">+</span>

<div id="accordion-item-51-panel" class="wp-block-accordion-panel is-layout-flow wp-block-accordion-panel-is-layout-flow" inert="" aria-labelledby="accordion-item-51" wp-bind--inert="!state.isOpen" role="region">

No. WordPress contributions are volunteer-based.

However, you gain:

- **Real-world open-source experience**
- **Practical, transferable skills**
- **Portfolio-worthy work**
- **Access to a global professional network**

Most importantly, you help improve WordPress for millions of users worldwide.

Contributing to WordPress is easier than it looks, and you are not expected to know everything from the start.

1.  **Start small**. Focus on learning the basics and getting comfortable with the process rather than trying to do everything at once. Small steps make it easier to build confidence and avoid feeling overwhelmed.\
2.  **Set up your accounts**. Take time to create and log in to the tools and platforms you will need so you can participate smoothly and follow along with your team.\
3.  **Pick a team**. Choose a team that aligns with your interests, skills, or curiosity. It is okay to join a team even if you are new and still learning.\
4.  **Complete one simple task before lunch**. Aim for a small, clearly defined task you can finish early in the day. That first contribution helps you understand the workflow and gives you a sense of momentum.

If you get stuck or feel unsure, ask for help. Open source thrives on collaboration, questions, and shared learning, and no one is expected to figure things out alone.
