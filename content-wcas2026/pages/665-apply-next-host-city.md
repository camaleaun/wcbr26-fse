WordCamp Asia is the flagship WordCamp of our region, where the WordPress community from across Asia and the world comes together to learn, share, and celebrate. Each year, a new city in Asia has the chance to welcome thousands of attendees, showcase its unique culture, and create memories that last a lifetime.

Now, it’s time to choose our next destination. **Applications are officially open for the host city of WordCamp Asia 2027!**

## Why Host WordCamp Asia?

Hosting WordCamp Asia is more than organizing an event, it’s about making your city the heart of the WordPress world for a few unforgettable days. Imagine:

- **Your city on the global stage.** Attendees from dozens of countries will experience your culture, food, and landmarks.
- **A boost for your local community.** Hosting strengthens your WordPress network, inspires new contributors, and builds momentum for years ahead.
- **Connections that last.** Organizers, sponsors, and attendees will carry friendships and memories from your city long after the event ends.

If your city has an enthusiastic WordPress community and the energy to bring people together, this is your chance.

## What We’re Looking For

To host a flagship WordCamp, your city should ideally have:

- A welcoming and active WordPress community with some past event experience (meetups or WordCamps).
- A venue big enough to welcome around **(attendee capacity).**
- Accessibility for both international and local travelers.
- A committed and diverse organizing team ready to collaborate with the global community.

Don’t worry if you’ve never hosted something this large before, you won’t be doing this alone. The global organizing team will be there to guide and support you every step of the way.
