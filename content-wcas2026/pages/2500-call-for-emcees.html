Why Become an Emcee?

WordCamp Asia isn’t just another conference. It’s a space where thousands of WordPress enthusiasts, developers, designers, and business owners gather to share, learn, and grow. As an emcee, you’ll be right at the heart of it all.

What you gain by taking the Mic?

Grab the mic, take the spotlight, and discover how this role can elevate your journey in the WordPress community!

  • Build visibility in the global WordPress community
  • Sharpen your public speaking and presentation skills
  • Connect with speakers, contributors, and industry leaders
  • Play a key role in making sessions flow smoothly

What are the Roles and Responsibilities of an Emcee?

Being an emcee means more than holding a microphone. You’ll set the tone for the event and help create a welcoming space for everyone.

Your role will include:

  • Introducing speakers and their topics with clarity and energy.
  • Keeping sessions on time and well-paced.
  • Engaging the audience and encouraging interaction.
  • Sharing key announcements and updates from the organizers.
  • Ensuring seamless transitions between talks, breaks, and panels.
  • Keeping the audience engaged in cases of technical issues or delays.

What We’re Looking For?

You don’t need to be a professional speaker to apply. What matters most is your confidence, presence, and enthusiasm for the community.

We’re looking for:

  • Friendly, approachable communicators.
  • Comfort speaking in front of a large, diverse audience.
  • Clear English, with the ability to connect across cultures.
  • Reliability and availability for rehearsals and assigned sessions.
  • Genuine passion for WordPress and open source.

How Selection Works?

We believe in fair opportunities and diverse representation. Here’s how we’ll select emcees for WordCamp Asia 2026:

  • Every application is reviewed with care.
  • We consider diversity across regions, languages, and backgrounds
  • Enthusiasm and commitment weigh as much as experience
  • Orientation and rehearsals will be scheduled before the event.
