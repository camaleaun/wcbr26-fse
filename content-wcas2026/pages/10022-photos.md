## Introduction

Do you love photography and want your work to travel the world without a passport? The Photos Team powers the WordPress Photo Directory, a growing library of free, high quality images that anyone can use.

When you contribute a photo, you are not just uploading a file. You are enabling bloggers, businesses, nonprofits, and creators to tell their stories visually on WordPress with confidence and credibility.

Whether you are a hobby photographer, a seasoned professional, or someone who simply enjoys capturing everyday moments, the Photos Team offers a welcoming entry point into contributing to WordPress.

Your lens. Someone else’s headline. That is impact.

## What Does the Photos Team Do?

At its core, the Photos Team makes sure the WordPress Photo Directory is filled with high-quality, freely licensed images.

As a new contributor, here are the main ways you can get involved:

- **Submit your photos:** Upload your original photos to the Photo Directory. Every approved photo helps others create blogs, websites, and projects without worrying about copyright issues.
- **Help with reviews (next step):** After gaining experience, you can learn to review photo submissions — checking for quality, guidelines, and ensuring each image is safe for public use.
- **Support the community:** Experienced members often guide new contributors, share tips, and collaborate with other WordPress teams to make the directory even better.

## Your Path as a Contributor

Contributing to the Photos Team is a journey, and you can grow at your own pace:

1.  **Start by submitting photos.** This is the easiest and most creative way to begin. Every photo you add makes the directory stronger.
2.  **Move into reviewing.** Once you’re familiar with the guidelines, you can start reviewing photo submissions and help ensure the collection maintains its quality.
3.  **Grow into leadership.** With experience, you might mentor new contributors, help shape submission guidelines, or even take on team roles.

No matter your level, your contributions are valuable and celebrated.

## Overcoming Challenges Together

Like any team, the Photos group sometimes faces challenges — things like balancing time, learning the review process, or contributing from different time zones. The good news? You’re never alone.

- You can contribute **at your own pace.**
- Experienced reviewers are always happy to guide you.
- Every contribution, big or small, matters.

## Why Join the Photos Team?

- **Learn and grow.** Improve your photography skills, understand licensing, and gain experience in reviewing.
- **Be part of something bigger.** Every contribution adds to the open-source spirit of WordPress.
