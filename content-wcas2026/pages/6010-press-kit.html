WordCamp Asia 2026 Overview

WordCamp Asia 2026 is the fourth edition of the flagship WordCamp event in Asia. 

After successful editions in Bangkok, Taipei, and Manila, this year’s event continues the spirit of an open-source web summit and welcomes agencies, creators, students, and digital professionals from different backgrounds.

The event will be held in Mumbai, India’s financial capital and the home of Bollywood. With more than 3,000 attendees expected, WordCamp Asia 2026 aims to bring together technology, creativity, and culture in one place.

Organized by a dedicated team of volunteers, WordCamp Asia 2026 aims to be inclusive, innovative, and meaningful for the global open-source community.
