Education is evolving — and so is the way we learn, contribute, and grow within the WordPress community.

At WordCamp Asia 2026, we’re bringing together three passionate leaders who are actively shaping how WordPress connects with students, educators, and aspiring contributors around the world.

About the Panel

Education Initiatives in the WordPress Ecosystem is a dynamic panel discussion focused on how open source and academia can work hand-in-hand.

This panel brings together leaders driving some of the most impactful education initiatives in the WordPress ecosystem — from Campus Connect programs and student clubs to structured contribution pathways and academic collaboration through WP Credits.

Meet the Panelists

Raitis Sevelis – Moderator
Product leader with 10+ years driving strategic growth for WordPress-focused B2B and B2C solutions, including WPBakery page builder. Deep expertise in e-commerce, UX, and scaling organizational teams. Active educator, speaker, and community contributor with a strong presence in IT and WordPress spaces.

Anand Upadhyay
Initiator of WordPress Campus Connect, Anand has been instrumental in bringing hands-on WordPress workshops to colleges and universities, helping students move from theory to real-world open source contribution.

Destiny Kanno
Head of Community Education at Automattic, Destiny leads efforts around structured learning pathways and community-driven education programs that connect learners with real contribution opportunities.

Maciej Pilarski
Maciej Pilarski (Matt) is a Community Wrangler at Automattic, contributing to WordPress.org and focusing on education and new contributors. He works on the WordPress Credits Program, connecting students with open source through university partnerships and mentorship.

What to Expect

This conversation will explore:

  • How WordPress integrates contributions into academic settings.
  • Building sustainable student communities.
  • Making open source more accessible to newcomers.
  • Supporting educators who want to teach WordPress.
  • The future of global WordPress education initiatives.

Whether you’re an educator, student, community organizer, or long-time contributor, this panel will offer insights into how WordPress is empowering learners worldwide — and how you can get involved.

Join us as we discuss the growing intersection of education and open source in the WordPress ecosystem.
