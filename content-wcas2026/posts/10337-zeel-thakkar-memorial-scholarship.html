Zeel Thakkar joined WordCamp Asia 2026 as a stellar contributor and WordPress community builder.

Losing Zeel was a big setback to the entire organising team, and to honor her memory, we are launching the Zeel Thakkar Memorial Scholarship. This is a fully funded opportunity for women in India and Nepal to attend WordCamp Asia 2026 in Mumbai (April 9–11).

What the Scholarship Covers

Each recipient will receive:

  • A free WordCamp Asia 2026 ticket
  • Reimbursement support for travel and accommodation for the duration of the conference

No out-of-pocket costs. You just show up and participate.

Who Should Apply

  • This scholarship is open to women from India and Nepal who are passionate about WordPress, open source, or web development.
  • We will prioritise folks who have not ever attended a flagship WordCamp (WordCamp Asia/ US/ EU) previously.
  • WordCamp Asia is a great way to start your WordPress journey. It can be career-defining. If you’ve been curious but never had the opportunity, this scholarship is for you.

Crowdfund to Help Us Make This Happen
