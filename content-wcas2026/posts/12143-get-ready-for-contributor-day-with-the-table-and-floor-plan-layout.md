Planning your **Contributor Day** experience at WordCamp Asia 2026 just got easier. We have now published the Table and Floor Plan Layout to help attendees get familiar with the space before arriving and find their way more easily on the day.

The layout gives you a clearer view of the Contributor Day setup, including where each team table is located and how the room is organized overall. Whether you already know which team you want to join or are still deciding, this can help you feel more prepared and confident before stepping into the room.

**Contributor Day** brings together people from many different backgrounds, interests, and experience levels. With a clearer floor plan and table arrangement, we hope it becomes easier for everyone to navigate the venue, locate the teams they are looking for, and move around the space more comfortably throughout the day.

We encourage all attendees to take a look at the layout in advance and use it as a quick guide during Contributor Day. It is a simple way to make your experience smoother, especially if this is your first time joining or if you want to plan your day ahead.
