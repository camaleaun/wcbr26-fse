WordCamp Asia 2026 in Mumbai is not just about sessions and selfies. It’s where career lightbulbs switch on.

Here are 5 conversations you’re definitely going to have. Be ready.

1. “I started in support.”

Someone will tell you they began by answering questions on the WordPress forums. They learned. They helped. They built confidence. Now they run agencies or work in product teams.

Action step: Start helping on support forums. It’s free education plus public credibility.

2. “My contributions became my portfolio.”

No fancy CV. No 5 page resume. Their proof of work is in core, plugins, docs, community.

Action step: Contribute. Even small commits matter. Visibility builds trust faster than cold pitching.

3. “Freelancing changed my life.”

Many started solo. Some stayed solo. Some built agencies. Not overnight success. Just consistent growth.

Action step: Talk to 3 freelancers at the event. Ask how they got their first client. Take notes.

4. “Our agency is built on community.”

The smartest agency owners here care about open source, ethical work, and long term brand building. They are not chasing quick money. They are building reputation capital.

Action step: If you run an agency, align with community values. It compounds.

5. “I decided to stay in WordPress.”

Big one. Many people don’t treat WordPress as a stepping stone. They grow within it. Mentor. Build. Lead.

Action step: Think long term. Skill depth beats random pivots.

Final Reality Check

This is not just a tech event. It’s a career accelerator disguised as a conference.
