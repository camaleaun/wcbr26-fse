This year’s WordPress Support Station is one of the best spots at WordCamp. It’s a welcoming space for learning, networking, and getting answers. For first-time attendees, it’s one of the most valuable places you can visit: a dedicated spot to ask questions and leave feeling more confident with WordPress.

What Can You Expect?

Think of it as WordCamp’s very own “Genius Bar”: a dedicated space where you can sit down with a WordPress expert to get one-on-one help with your website.

Get unstuck. From e-commerce setup to Block Editor questions, you can walk through CSS tweaks, HTML basics, troubleshooting issues, and everything in between.


Want to install a plugin but aren’t sure how? Curious about a new theme? Looking to solve some code issues or get security advice? Just starting and need help getting your homepage up and running? This station is the place to do it.

Who Runs the Support Station?


The amazing WordCamp volunteers. They hail from all kinds of backgrounds: developers, designers, bloggers, business owners, and yes, even some of our event speakers. They staff the station throughout the event, ready to help with all things WordPress. Not every problem can be solved on the spot, but you’ll always leave with direction, resources, or a next step.

Learn from these experts. They’ll help point you in the right direction, share resources, and get hands on with your WordPress site to find you the solution you’re looking for.

Where Can You Find It?
Location: The Sponsor Hall, Jasmine 2
Hours of Operation: 10:00 am- 5:00 pm on Friday, April 10, and Saturday, April 11.


Bring your curiosity, and see you there!
