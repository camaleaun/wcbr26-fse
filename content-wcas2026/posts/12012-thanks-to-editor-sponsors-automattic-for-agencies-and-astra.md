Astra follows clean coding standards and is optimized for real-world performance, helping websites achieve fast load times and strong Core Web Vitals. With 300+ ready-to-use Starter Templates and seamless integration with popular page builders and WooCommerce, Astra makes it easy to build business websites, online stores, portfolios, and client projects of any size.
