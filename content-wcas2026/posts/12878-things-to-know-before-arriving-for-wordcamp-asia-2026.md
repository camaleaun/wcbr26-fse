## e-Arrival Card – Mandatory for All Foreign Nationals

⚠️ **Required before you land in India.** All foreign nationals entering India must complete the e-Arrival Card online. This must be done within **72 hours before your arrival**. We suggest not leaving this until the last minute.

### How to Register?

Visit the e-Arrival Card website and complete your form. The form is free and takes approximately 5 minutes.

1.  Visit the official portal: <a href="https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/earrival/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">indianvisaonline.gov.in/earrival</a>.
2.  Complete the form within **72 hours before your flight lands in India.**
3.  Save a screenshot or download the confirmation, as you may be asked to present it at immigration.
4.  Keep it accessible offline on your phone in PDF format or an image at the airport.

### Who Needs to Register?

- All foreign nationals entering India, regardless of nationality.
- This applies to tourists, business travelers, speakers, sponsors, and volunteers.
- Indian nationals do not need to complete this form.

**Tip:** *Set a calendar reminder to fill this in 72 hours before your arrival time. If you have a layover, your arrival time is when you land in India, not when you depart from your home country.*

## Getting to JWCC – Travel and Accommodation

### The Venue

Jio World Convention Centre is located in **Bandra-Kurla Complex (BKC)**, Mumbai’s central business district. BKC is well-connected by road, metro, and auto-rickshaw and is one of the most accessible parts of the city for visitors.

- **By metro:** Bandra Kurla Complex (BKC) station on Metro Line 3 Aqua Line is a short walk from the Jio World Convention Centre (JWCC) Gate 18. 
- **By road:** Approximately 30 – 45 minutes from Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (CSIA), depending on traffic. App-based cabs (Uber, Ola) are widely available.
- **By auto-rickshaw:** Available within Bandra Kurla Complex (BKC) for short distances.

To know more about the venue, [check this blog post](https://asia.wordcamp.org/2026/about-the-venue/). 

### Event Shuttle

- We are offering a complimentary WordCamp Asia Event Shuttle. This will help serve our attendees who are staying at both our official event hotels, the Trident & Sofitel, during the event, and also to/from the Bandra Kurla Complex (BKC) Metro station.
- Shuttles will depart/return to both hotels, (BKC) Metro station, and the Jio Convention Centre during WordCamp hours only from Contributor Day through the Closing Party. 
- Please remember to wear your event badge to allow you to access this service.

### Where to Stay

You can check out the [official hotels](https://asia.wordcamp.org/2026/official-hotels/) and the [nearby hotels](https://asia.wordcamp.org/2026/hotel-choices/) as per your convenience.

### Come Prepared for Mumbai Weather

April is summer in Mumbai, so expect warm and humid conditions.

- **Temperature:** 33- 36°C (91- 97°F) during the day.
- **Humidity:** High-breathable fabrics make a real difference.
- **Indoors:** JWCC is air-conditioned — bring a light layer for long sessions inside.
