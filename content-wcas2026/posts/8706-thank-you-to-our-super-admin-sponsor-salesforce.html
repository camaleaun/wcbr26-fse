Salesforce is globally recognized as a leader in customer relationship management (CRM) and enterprise cloud solutions. Salesforce is also a powerful ally of the open-source community, and  their support of WordCamp US speaks volumes.

Salesforce’s relationship with WordPress goes far beyond sponsorship. Each year, Salesforce donates $8.8 million USD to support the Make/WordPress Slack instance, the central hub for real-time collaboration across the WordPress project. This generous contribution ensures that contributors around the world have access to premium Slack features, enabling smoother communication, faster decision-making, and a more connected global community.

From core developers to documentation teams, this support helps directly fuel WordPress.

Salesforce‘s cloud-based platforms, Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, and Marketing Cloud, are powered by AI-driven tools that help businesses make smarter, data-informed decisions. But what makes their sponsorship of WordCamp US truly special is their belief in open innovation.

By backing WordCamp, Salesforce is investing in the creators, developers, and educators who shape the future of the web. It’s a powerful reminder that enterprise and open-source can thrive together.

Thank you, Salesforce, for helping power WordCamp Asia 2026 in Mumbai. Your contributions help keep our community connected, our tools collaborative, and our future bright.
