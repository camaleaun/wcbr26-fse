Documents Required

To purchase a prepaid SIM card in India, keep the following ready:

  • Original passport
  • Valid Indian visa (including e‑visa)
  • Passport‑size photo (often optional)
  • Local address (hotel address works)

All SIM activations require identity verification (KYC).

Where to Buy

At Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport

Purchasing a SIM at Mumbai’s international airport is usually the simplest option. Available providers commonly include Airtel, Jio, and Vodafone Idea.

Typical prepaid tourist packs may include:

  • 1–2 GB daily data
  • Unlimited local calls
  • SMS access

Approximate price range: ₹500–₹1,000 INR depending on plan validity and data allowance.

Advantages of airport purchase:

  • Staff familiar with international travellers
  • Faster documentation handling
  • Reduced activation delays

City Store Option

You can also visit official telecom stores in Mumbai. Prices may be slightly cheaper. Activation may take longer. Avoid roadside sellers. If it looks informal, it probably is.

Activation Timeline

  • Airport SIM: Often active within a few hours
  • City SIM: Can take up to 24 hours

Buy during daytime. Night activations sometimes move slower.

If it does not activate:

• Restart your phone
• Check SMS permissions
• Turn off international roaming

Simple fixes solve most issues.

eSIM Alternative

If your phone supports eSIM, you can activate data instantly. Some plans are data only. That means no local number, which can be inconvenient for OTP verification. If convenience is your priority, physical SIM is still safer.

Internet Access and Wi‑Fi in Mumbai

Internet Access (Mobile Data)

This is your most reliable option.

You will need it for:

• Google Maps
• Uber or Ola bookings
• Event updates
• Ticket confirmations
• UPI payments

Prepaid plans usually give daily high speed data. More than enough for navigation, video calls, and social media updates.

Airport Wi‑Fi

Available at Mumbai Airport. You must enter a mobile number to receive an OTP. Local SIM makes this seamless. If OTP does not arrive, ask the help desk for a temporary voucher.

Hotel Wi‑Fi

Most hotels offer it. Reality check: Speed depends on how many guests are streaming at the same time. Use it as backup. Not as your main lifeline.

Cafés and Public Hotspots

Common in malls and commercial areas. Often require OTP login. Public WiFi is less secure. Use mobile data for payments.

Reliability Comparison

Mobile data remains the most dependable option and generally offers better consistency, speed, and security compared with public Wi‑Fi networks.

For WC Asia attendees, WordCamp Asia venue Wi‑Fi is typically available and useful for quick browsing or uploads, but having mobile data is safer.

Using UPI Payments in India

What is UPI

UPI (Unified Payments Interface) is India’s real‑time digital payment system that enables instant transfers through QR codes and mobile apps.

UPI is widely accepted across India — from retail stores to taxis and restaurants.

UPI One World Wallet

UPI One World is designed to help international visitors make UPI payments without needing an Indian bank account.

Setup Overview

  1. Install a supported UPI One World app
  2. Register using your international mobile number
  3. Visit an authorised verification counter
  4. Complete identity verification (valid passport + visa)
  5. Load INR via international card
  6. Set payment PIN
  7. Begin scanning QR codes

Where It Works

UPI QR payments are accepted at most merchants, cafés, shops, and transport providers.

Balance & Refunds

Unused wallet balance can typically be refunded through authorised counters before departure.
