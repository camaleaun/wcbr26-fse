Contributor Day is the heart and soul of WordCamp Asia, a day filled with hands-on collaboration, creativity, and community spirit that opens the entire event. Whether you are a seasoned WordPress enthusiast or finding it difficult to getting started,Contributor Day welcomes everyone to participate, learn, and make an impact.

A Day of meaningful contribution

Contributor Day is not just another meetup. It is a hands-on experience where you actively contribute to WordPress, the open source project that powers over 43% of the web. This is where ideas become action and where your curiosity becomes real progress.

No matter your background, design, writing, development, marketing, accessibility, translation, documentation, or testing, there is a place for you. You will work alongside global contributors and see your efforts make a genuine difference.

Learn through doing

Unlike typical conference sessions, Contributor Day is hands on. You can choose from various Make WordPress teams and contribute directly to areas such as:

  • Writing or improving documentation
  • Helping with translations
  • Testing and debugging issues
  • Design and accessibility
  • Community outreach and more

Experienced contributors and Table Leads are present throughout the day. This support makes it easy for first timers to get started and for returning contributors to deepen their impact.

Build the connections

Contributor Day brings together passionate people from all corners of the world. It offers opportunities to:

  • Network with WordPress professionals and mentors
  • Build friendships and find collaborations
  • Discover paths you may not have known existed within the open source ecosystem
  • Share ideas, insights, experiences, and personal journeys with peers 

Many attendees share that the relationships they form on a Contributor Day influence their careers, open new doors, and inspire long term growth.

The Open-Source Library 

One of the highlights of Contributor Day 2026 is the Open-Source Library, which builds on the concept previously known as the Human Library.

At the Open-Source Library, you can have one on one conversations with experienced professionals known as Open-Source Books. These experts openly share their journeys, lessons, challenges, and insights from working in open source.

What makes this experience meaningful:

One to one learning
Instead of reading about experiences, you engage directly with the people whose stories offer practical and lived wisdom.

Broader conversations
Discussions go beyond contribution paths and include leadership, workflows, career growth, community building, and collaboration.

This space encourages curiosity, connection, and meaningful dialogue that often sparks new ideas and motivation.✨

Everyone is welcome

Contributor Day is inclusive by design. You do not need special skills or years of experience. Curiosity, openness, and willingness to participate are enough. Support and guidance are always available, whether you are contributing for the first time or returning again.

Be the part of something greater

By attending Contributor Day, you become part of a global community working together for the betterment of the web. Every contribution matters and helps strengthen the WordPress and open source ecosystem.It is an experience that shapes your journey and contributes to the future of open source.

Contributor Day at WordCamp Asia 2026 is waiting for you. Bring your curiosity, connect with passionate people, and make your mark on WordPress.

How to prepare for WCAsia2026

A little preparation can help you get the most out of WordCamp Asia, especially Contributor Day.

Register early
WordCamp Asia attracts attendees from around the world and passes sales quickly. Early registration helps you plan your schedule and participation comfortably. Grab your pass if you haven’t secured your spot yet.

Understand the event structure
Familiarize yourself with Contributor Day, conference days, and community activities so you can decide where to focus your time and energy.

Plan for conversations
Make time to visit the Open-Source Library. Prepare a few questions or topics you are curious about. These conversations often become highlights of the day.

Stay connected
Follow WordCamp Asia channels to stay informed and begin connecting with fellow attendees before the event.
