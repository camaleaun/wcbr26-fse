The stage is set. The timer is ready. And now, it’s time to meet the builders 👇

We’re excited to introduce the **two contestants** who will take on the **WordPress Speed Build Challenge** at **WordCamp Asia 2026**.

### ⚡ Ajay Maurya

*Ajay is a Senior Frontend Engineer at [Fueled](https://fueled.com), where he has spent the last five years building enterprise-scale WordPress solutions. He specialises in Full Site Editing and block-theme development, and has contributed to several open-source repositories. Ajay is an active organiser and speaker in the [Mumbai WordPress community](https://wpmumbai.org), as well as part of the Technology Team in WordCamp Asia 2026.*

### ⚡ Craig Gomes

*Craig is the founder of [Pixelvise](http://Pixelvise), where he handles everything from UI/UX and theme architecture to performance optimisation and deployment. He is also a WordPress educator and has [covered](https://www.youtube.com/@craiggomes/videos) FSE builds, custom systems, and advanced workflows on his YouTube channel. Craig runs his personal site on a headless WordPress setup powered by a custom RAG-based AI chatbot. A geek at heart!*

Ajay and Craig two will go head-to-head in a **live**<span style="margin: 0px;padding: 0px">** 30-minute challenge to** build</span> a WordPress website using **only the Full Site Editor (FSE)**, right in front of a global audience.

Guiding the challenge live on stage will be **[Jamie Marsland](https://profiles.wordpress.org/jamesmarsland/)**, bringing energy, insights, and keeping the pressure on as the clock ticks.

### 🔥 What to expect

- Real-time website building
- Fast decisions and creative problem-solving
- A high-energy, high-pressure showdown

Who will build faster? Who will build smarter? We’ll find out live on stage.
