The Open-Source Library returns to WordCamp Asia 2026 Contributor Day as a space for meaningful one-on-one conversations shaped by real experiences in open source. Inspired by the Human Library tradition from previous editions(2024 & 2025), it invites attendees to learn from people through honest and thoughtful dialogue.

Explore the Open-Source Books below and experience contributors and community members who share their journeys, insights, and lessons learned. Discover the stories, perspectives, and experiences they are ready to share, browse their topics and available time slots, and choose a conversation that speaks to your interests, questions, or curiosity.

Curious to learn more? Explore the official Open-Source Library page to see how the program works, who can participate, and what to expect during Contributor Day.
