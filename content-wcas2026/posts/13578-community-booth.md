WordCamp Asia 2026 is kicking off, and the Community Booth is ready to welcome attendees from around the world.

If you’re looking to explore the WordPress community, discover contribution opportunities, or simply ask questions, this is the place to start.

## What is the Community Booth?

The Community Booth is a dedicated space where WordCamp attendees can:

- Learn about different WordPress teams and initiatives
- Ask questions about community programs
- Discover ways to contribute and grow
- Connect with experienced community members

It’s designed to be approachable, informative, and welcoming—whether you’re new to WordPress or already involved.

Located in the sponsor hall, the booth will be open on **Friday, April 10** and **Saturday, April 11**, with one-hour sessions covering a range of community topics. Whether you are curious about WordPress Credits, Campus Connect, meetups, contribution pathways, or education programs, there is something for you.

## Why You Should Visit

- Get guidance on how to start contributing
- Learn about global and local WordPress initiatives
- Meet friendly community members
- Find opportunities to grow your skills and network

Whether you’re curious or committed, there’s something valuable for everyone.

## Stop By and Say Hello

Drop by the Community Booth in the sponsor hall whenever you have a free moment. Have a question about a session topic? Feel free to reach out to the listed contact person or simply visit during the scheduled time.

The community members at the booth look forward to meeting you. See you at WordCamp Asia!
