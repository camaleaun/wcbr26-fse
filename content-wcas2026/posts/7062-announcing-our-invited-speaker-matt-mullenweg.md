## About Ma.tt Mullenweg

Ma.tt Mullenweg is the co-founder of **WordPress**, the open source platform that powers **over 43 percent of websites** across the internet. He is also the **founder and CEO of Automattic**, the company behind products like **WordPress.com, WooCommerce, Jetpack, Tumblr, Pocket Casts,** and more, with a strong mission to make the web a better place.

Through his work, Ma.tt has focused on a simple but powerful goal: **democratising publishing, commerce, and messaging**, so more people can build, share, and grow online. At **WordCamp US 2025**, he delivered the closing keynote on the **future of WordPress and AI**, sharing innovations around AI-powered tools, WordPress core updates, and what the open web needs next.

Ma.tt is known not only as a tech leader but also as a creative person, with interests in photography and music. He continues to shape the WordPress ecosystem globally through his leadership, ideas, and community-driven approach
