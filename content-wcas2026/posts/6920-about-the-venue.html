Jio World Convention Centre

Jio World Convention Centre (JWCC) was selected as the venue for WordCamp Asia 2026 based on its ability to support a large-scale, international WordPress event while meeting the logistical, accessibility, and community needs of a flagship WordCamp.

Located in Mumbai, JWCC is a purpose-built convention centre that regularly hosts global conferences, exhibitions, and cultural events, making it well suited for an event of this scale.

A space built for scale

JWCC offers expansive, column-free halls with flexible and modular layouts. This allows WordCamp Asia to host multiple session tracks, workshops, sponsor areas, Contributor Day activities, and community spaces within a single venue. The layout also supports clear navigation and smooth crowd movement for a high number of attendees.

World class infrastructure

The venue is equipped with modern audiovisual systems, reliable on-site technical infrastructure, and high-capacity internet connectivity—essential requirements for WordCamp Asia. Its design prioritises accessibility, safety, and efficient crowd flow, helping ensure a smooth and comfortable experience for attendees throughout the event. JWCC stands out globally for its world-class infrastructure, including one of the world’s largest passenger elevators.

Location that means business

Choosing JWCC was not just about logistics. It was about hosting WordCamp Asia 2026 in a venue that symbolises innovation, ambition, and global standards. The same values the WordPress ecosystem champions.

Designed for the community

WordCamp events are centred around collaboration and conversation. JWCC includes open lounges, shared spaces, and quieter areas that support informal discussions, networking, and spontaneous meetups, an important part of the WordCamp experience and the WordPress open-source community.

Future ready and iconic

JWCC represents Mumbai’s growing role as a global destination for large-scale technology and community events. Its modern design and international standards align well with the scale and expectations of WordCamp Asia 2026, while supporting the inclusive and community-driven values of WordCamp events.

Getting There & Transportation Options

The Jio World Convention Centre is located in Bandra Kurla Complex (BKC), one of Mumbai’s best-connected business districts.

Address: Jio World Centre, G Block, Bandra Kurla Complex, Bandra East, Mumbai, Maharashtra 400098

Get Map Directions

By Air

  • The nearest airport is Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM).
  • Distance: ~6–7 km
  • Travel time: 20–30 minutes by road (can be longer during peak hours)
  • Recommended option: App-based taxi or hotel-arranged cab
  • Both domestic and international terminals are well connected to BKC, making this the most convenient arrival option for out-of-city and international attendees.

By Metro and Local Transport

Metro

The closest metro access is via Bandra Kurla Complex Metro Station on Mumbai Metro Line 3 (Aqua Line).

From the metro station, the venue is a short walk or cab ride. Metro is reliable and avoids road traffic during peak hours.

Please note: mobile network connectivity may be intermittent during the metro ride.

Local Trains (Suburban Railway)

Nearby major stations include Bandra, Kurla, and Santacruz.

  • From these stations, taxis, auto-rickshaws (where permitted), and buses are easily available
  • Local trains are fast but can be crowded during rush hours

By Air

BKC is well connected by major arterial roads and city expressways.

  • Taxis and ride-share services are the most convenient option
  • BEST buses operate frequently in and around BKC, with stops near the venue. Chalo Bus – S102 Bandra East to ONGC BKC and S180 BKC Metro to ONGC
  • The nearest major bus access point is Bandra Bus Depot, which connects multiple parts of the city

Designated drop-off and pick-up points for WordCamp Asia 2026 will be shared closer to the event to help manage traffic efficiently.

Venue Access & Entry Points

At the Jio World Convention Centre, designated access points will be used to ensure smooth crowd movement during WordCamp Asia 2026.

  • Entry Gate: Gate No. 18
  • Exit Gate: Gate No. 20

Clear signage and volunteer support will be available to guide attendees from these gates to registration and session areas.

Parking

  • Basement parking is available at the venue
  • Parking is offered on a prepaid basis, subject to availability
  • Attendees are encouraged to consider public transport or ride-share services where possible, especially during peak hours

Final parking instructions, drop-off points, and pedestrian routes will be shared closer to the event dates and aligned with the official venue access plan.
