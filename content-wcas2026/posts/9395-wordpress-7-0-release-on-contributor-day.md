**WordPress 7.0 was originally planned for release on Contributor Day at WordCamp Asia 2026 in Mumbai.** However, the release **has been slightly postponed** to give the team additional time to fine-tune advanced features like real-time collaboration and to ensure the release meets high standards of stability and quality.\
Read official announcement – <a href="https://make.wordpress.org/core/2026/03/31/extending-the-7-0-cycle/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Extending the 7.0 cycle</a>

</div>

</div>

To keep the momentum alive, the event will now feature **WordPress Release Panel Discussions and an Open Floor**, giving contributors and attendees a chance to dive into what’s coming, share feedback, and engage directly with the people shaping the release.

WordPress 7.0 will reflects months of collective effort from contributors around the world. Every release is made possible by the dedication, team work, and care of several hundred individuals. Contributor Day is a final opportunity to collaborate and celebrate every contribution in new and meaningful ways.

Attendees at Contributor Day events represent diverse skillsets, backgrounds, and experience levels. Releasing a major version of WordPress during the event presents a ton of unique and engaging programming opportunities that we can’t wait for you to participate in.

If you’re planning to attend Contributor Day, here’s just a few things that you can look forward to:

- Some members of the WordPress 7.0 release squad will be present at the event.
- Select tables will be focused on supporting the release process, especially through testing..
- **Morning sessions will focus on explaining how the WordPress release cycle works and how to get involved.**
- **Chances to engage directly with contributors involved in the release, understand how changes move forward, and experience the spirit of open-source teamwork firsthand.**

You’ll be able to observe, ask questions, and contribute alongside experienced contributors throughout the day. Whether you’re new or returning, the day is being designed to offer a variety of valuable workshops and topics.
