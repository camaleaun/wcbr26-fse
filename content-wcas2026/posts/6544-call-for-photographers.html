Your photography toolkit

To deliver professional results in a fast-paced environment, come prepared.

  • Camera(s): A high-end DSLR or mirrorless body with excellent low-light performance. Pro Tip: Bring a backup body.
  • Lenses:  A standard zoom (e.g., 24-70mm), or a fast prime (f/1.4 or f/1.8) for those low-light portraits, or a telephoto (e.g., 70-200mm) for capturing subjects from a distance.
  • Power & Storage: High-capacity memory cards, backup storage, enough spare batteries and chargers to power you through an 8-hour Mumbai day.
  • Lighting & Stability: External flash units for indoor control and a sturdy tripod for those long-exposure crowd shots.
