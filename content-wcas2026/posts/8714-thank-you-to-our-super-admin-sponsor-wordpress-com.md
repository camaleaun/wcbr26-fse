We’re delighted to welcome **WordPress.com** as a **Super Admin Sponsor** of **WordCamp Asia 2026**, happening this April in Mumbai.

WordPress.com helps millions of people around the world bring their ideas to life online, from personal blogs and creative portfolios to growing businesses and global publications. Built on the open-source WordPress project, WordPress.com makes publishing more accessible, more secure, and more empowering for creators everywhere.

At its heart, WordPress has always been about community: people coming together to share knowledge, build tools, and support one another. WordPress.com plays an important role in that ecosystem by helping new users enter the WordPress world and experienced creators scale their impact.

As a Super Admin Sponsor, WordPress.com’s support helps us create a space where the global WordPress community can:

- Learn from one another through shared stories and experiences.
- Contribute back to the open-source project we all care about.
- Connect across cultures, regions, and disciplines.
- Strengthen the relationships that power the web.

WordCamp Asia is more than an event;  it’s a gathering of contributors, designers, developers, entrepreneurs, and creators who believe in an open web.

We’re grateful to WordPress.com for supporting that mission and helping bring the community together in Mumbai.
