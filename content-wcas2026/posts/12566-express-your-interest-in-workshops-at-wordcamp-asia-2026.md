Our workshops are interactive, practical, and led by experienced speakers from across the global WordPress community. You’ll get the chance to:

- Connect with fellow attendees

## **Important Points Before Showing Your Interest**

- **Valid Ticket Required:** You must have a **WordCamp Asia 2026 ticket** and register with the same email. Mismatched registrations will be removed.
- **Workshop  Requirements:** Some workshops may require **specific software or tools**. A **laptop is mandatory** for all sessions. Any additional requirements will be emailed to you a week before the event.
- **Arrive on Time:** Arrive **at least 10 minutes early** to secure your seat. After this time, unclaimed spots will be given to attendees waiting outside the room.
- **Important: Registration does not guarantee you a seat.** The entry will be on a first-come basis. 

## **How to Works**

Workshops have limited seats. Share your interest through the form; entries will be considered on a first-come basis.
