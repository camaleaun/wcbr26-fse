We are excited to unveil the **tenth round of speakers** for **WordCamp Asia 2026**.

This milestone round reflects the strength and diversity of our global WordPress community. From developers and designers to marketers, educators, and community leaders, these speakers represent the many paths that shape how WordPress is built and experienced around the world.

Their sessions will focus on real challenges, practical solutions, and lessons learned through hands-on work. Expect meaningful insights, thoughtful discussions, and ideas you can apply to your own projects, teams, and communities.

With ten rounds now announced, WordCamp Asia 2026 is coming together as a space for learning that matters, connections that last, and conversations that move the ecosystem forward.

Stay tuned as we continue unveiling the voices shaping WordPress across Asia and beyond. In the meantime, revisit our [speakers lineup](https://asia.wordcamp.org/2026/speakers/) to catch up on all the incredible announcements so far, and don’t forget to [secure your pass](https://asia.wordcamp.org/2026/event-pass/?refresh=true#get-your-pass) to be part of the experience.
