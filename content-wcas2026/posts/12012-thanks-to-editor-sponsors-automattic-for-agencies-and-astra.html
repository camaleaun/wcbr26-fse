Astra

Astra is one of the world’s most widely used WordPress themes, powering over 1.8 million websites. It’s built to be fast, stable, and flexible – giving creators and developers a lightweight foundation to build professional sites with confidence.

Astra follows clean coding standards and is optimized for real-world performance, helping websites achieve fast load times and strong Core Web Vitals. With 300+ ready-to-use Starter Templates and seamless integration with popular page builders and WooCommerce, Astra makes it easy to build business websites, online stores, portfolios, and client projects of any size.

Astra is part of the Brainstorm Force ecosystem, a unified suite of WordPress solutions covering site creation, optimization, automation, and monetization. The ecosystem includes tools like Spectra, ZipWP, CartFlows, SureCart, SureRank, SureForms, Sigmize, OttoKit, and more.

Trusted by freelancers, agencies, and businesses worldwide, Brainstorm Force is building tools that help WordPress sites grow and scale with clarity and control.
