WordCamp Asia 2026 is happening on April 9–11 in Mumbai at the Jio World Convention Centre, and we want to make sure students can be part of it.

We’re introducing a dedicated [student pass priced **at ₹1,000/-**](https://asia.wordcamp.org/2026/event-pass/?refresh=true#get-your-pass)**\**

### **What’s Included**

- Entry for both conference days
- Full Access to all workshops
- Contributor Day (requires separate registration)
- Check out the released <a href="https://asia.wordcamp.org/2026/schedule/" data-type="page" data-id="7">schedule here</a>.

**What’s Not Included**

Student Pass will not include entry to After Party on the final day of WordCamp Asia.\

### **Why ₹1,000/-?**

We wanted a price point that is low enough to be accessible, high enough to feel like a commitment. ₹1,000/- hits that mark. 

Workshops are a big part of what makes WordCamp Asia valuable, and student passes include full access to them. If you’ve been waiting for a hands-on way to get into WordPress, this is it.

**How to use the Students Discount?**

Head over to the event pass registration link, and you will find three options, namely Pro Pass, Standard Pass, and Student Pass.

Choose the Student Pass, purchase it using UPI or any other method, and we will see you at WordCamp Asia 2026 in Mumbai in April!
