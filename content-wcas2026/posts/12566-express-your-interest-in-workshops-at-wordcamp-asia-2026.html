Important Points Before Showing Your Interest

  • Valid Ticket Required: You must have a WordCamp Asia 2026 ticket and register with the same email. Mismatched registrations will be removed.

  • Arrive on Time: Arrive at least 10 minutes early to secure your seat. After this time, unclaimed spots will be given to attendees waiting outside the room.
  • Important: Registration does not guarantee you a seat. The entry will be on a first-come basis.
