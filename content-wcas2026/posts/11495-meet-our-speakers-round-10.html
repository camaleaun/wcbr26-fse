We are excited to unveil the tenth round of speakers for WordCamp Asia 2026.

This milestone round reflects the strength and diversity of our global WordPress community. From developers and designers to marketers, educators, and community leaders, these speakers represent the many paths that shape how WordPress is built and experienced around the world.
