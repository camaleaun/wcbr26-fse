Marketing hasn’t just evolved; it has **shifted rapidly in a very short time**, and AI is at the center of this change. It’s no longer just a tool; it’s transforming how content is created, discovered, and measured.

At **WordCamp Asia**, this panel goes beyond the usual AI buzz. Expect a **ground-level, experience-driven conversation** focused on what is actually changing in marketing today.

💡 **What to expect:**

- How AI is reshaping SEO, content strategy, and discoverability
- What’s still driving results — and what has quietly stopped working
- Where marketers may be wasting time without realizing it
- How teams, workflows, and roles are evolving in the AI era
- Real examples of what has been tested, adapted, and proven

Whether you’re a **product owner, marketer, or agency leader**, this session will help you cut through the noise and understand what to do next.
