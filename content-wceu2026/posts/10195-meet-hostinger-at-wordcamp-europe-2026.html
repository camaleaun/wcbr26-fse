This year at WordCamp Europe, we’re proud to be supported by an incredible group of partners who have helped bring WCEU to life and shape it into the event so many of you love. That’s why we’re delighted to shine a spotlight on some of our biggest sponsors here on the blog.

Now, let’s hand over the mic to Hostinger, one of our Editor Level sponsors!

From June 4–6, the WordPress community will gather in Kraków, Poland, for WordCamp Europe 2026, and Hostinger will be there as a sponsor.

WordCamp Europe is one of the most important events in the WordPress ecosystem, bringing together developers, agencies, contributors, and site owners from across the region and beyond. We’re excited to be part of it and to connect with the community in Kraków.

At Hostinger, WordPress is a major focus. Our managed hosting for WordPress is built around an agentic WordPress experience that helps guide users through every stage of the journey, from creating a site to launching, maintaining, and growing it. Our goal is simple: make WordPress easier to build, easier to run, and more accessible to everyone.

At our booth, attendees will be able to learn more about how we’re shaping a more guided, user-friendly WordPress experience, including chat-based site creation and managed hosting that is fast, secure, and hands-off.

But for us, WordCamp is about more than products. It’s about listening, learning, and supporting the open-source community behind WordPress.

If you’re attending WordCamp Europe 2026, stop by and say hello. We’d love to meet you in Kraków and talk about the future of WordPress.
