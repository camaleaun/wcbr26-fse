Excited to enjoy the community spirit along with your little one at WordCamp Europe 2026? Children of all ages are welcome at the event. We ask that they not be left unaccompanied, and that you try to minimise disruption during talks and workshops.

Want to be able to focus on the day more? We are thrilled to say that free childcare will be available during Contributor Day and the main conference days, from 4 to 6 June 2026, during the scheduled event hours.

Childcare services will be provided by a professional contractor and experienced childcare staff, following Polish regulations.

We are currently at full capacity. You may check if any spaces have become available directly at the venue during the event, but we cannot guarantee admission.

Childcare Information

Note: Information is provisional and may vary. We’ll email all registrants if there are any major updates.

  • Childcare is available only for children ages 3 to 10.
  • No food will be allowed in the childcare room. Only water and other beverages are permitted.
  • Mealtimes remain the responsibility of the accompanying adult, who will need to take charge of the child for any meals.
  • No sharp or dangerous objects are allowed in the childcare room.
  • Only healthy children are allowed in the childcare area. 
  • Parents or guardians must provide all necessary information about their child, including allergies, special needs, and contact details to reach them if needed.
  • If a child becomes unwell or starts feeling homesick, the accompanying adult will be contacted right away by text message or phone call using the number provided at registration.
  • Children will be registered at the entrance, along with the accompanying adult’s name and contact details.
  • Collection must be done by the same accompanying adult who completed the registration, and the child must be signed for.
  • The accompanying adult who drops off and picks up the child is required to present an identity document.
  • Animators do not administer any medications to children. A basic first-aid kit is available in the building, and if an injury or accident occurs, the childcare coordinator contacts the accompanying adult as well as the medic provided by the organizer.
  • If the child uses disposable diapers, the accompanying adult must provide hygiene supplies and sign consent for the animator to change the diaper. If consent is not given, we will contact the accompanying adult by phone and ask them to come.
  • It is strictly forbidden for the accompanying adult to leave the event while the child is in the childcare room.
  • In the event of an emergency requiring evacuation, childcare staff will guide the child safely to the outdoor gathering point and continue to care for them there. The accompanying adult will be contacted immediately by text message or phone call and informed that the child has been moved to the outside collection point.

Childcare will be available from 15 minutes before the first session starts until 15 minutes after the last session ends. It will not be available during the After-Party.

As plans continue to come together, this information may change. If there are any major updates, we will email everyone who has registered for childcare.

Psst… You don’t need to buy a conference ticket for your children unless you want them to have their own badge, lunch, and other conference goodies.

We hope this helps make attending WordCamp Europe 2026 a little easier for families. We look forward to welcoming both you and your young champs!
