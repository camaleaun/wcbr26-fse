What to know before you register?

Spaces Fill Up: Seats are limited to keep workshops manageable and engaging. Once a workshop is full, registration closes. But don’t worry, there’s a queuing system outside the workshop room on the day, and if someone doesn’t show up, you get in. Fair and square.

Some Workshops Have Tech Requirements: Most are hands-on, so you might need a laptop, specific software installed, or an account set up beforehand. We’ve listed exactly what each workshop needs, so you can check whether it’s a good fit for your setup.

Show Up Early: If you pre-registered, arrive at least 10 minutes early. We want to make sure you get a seat and have time to settle in before things kick off.

You Can Still Cancel: Plans change, and that’s okay. If you need to cancel your registration, just contact our Speakers Team.
