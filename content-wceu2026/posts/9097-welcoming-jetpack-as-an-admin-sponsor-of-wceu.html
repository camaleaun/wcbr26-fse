Jetpack: The Complete WordPress Toolkit — Now More Powerful, More Intuitive

Jetpack is the most comprehensive way to make your WordPress site faster, more secure, and easier to grow. Whether you’re a solo blogger, an agency managing dozens of client sites, or an enterprise publisher, Jetpack gives you the tools to ship with confidence — all built natively into WordPress.

In 2026, we’ve been focused on three pillars: a modern, unified experience, AI-powered productivity, and rock-solid security and performance.

We’ve completely refreshed Jetpack’s interface with cleaner layouts, clearer settings, and a more consistent design across every feature. It feels like the WordPress-native toolkit it always should have been — easy to understand, easy to try.

On the AI front, Jetpack AI now powers a unified sidebar with title optimization, content coaching, and image generation — all integrated where you write. We’re also bringing MCP (Model Context Protocol) support to Jetpack, making it possible for AI agents to interact with your site natively.

Under the hood, Jetpack continues to deliver real-time backups, automated malware scanning, brute force protection, downtime monitoring, and CDN-powered performance. Jetpack Boost gives you one-click speed improvements, and Jetpack Social now supports sharing to X with a smart quota system alongside your existing social connections.

We’ve also shipped major improvements to Jetpack Forms with a new editor experience, webhook integrations, and WooCommerce search filters in Instant Search.
