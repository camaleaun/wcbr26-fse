## **About the Venue: Forty Kleparz**

Forty Kleparz sits on the northern edge of Kraków’s Old Town inside a 19th-century brick fortification — part of the city’s old Austro-Hungarian defensive ring. Today its vaulted interiors, thick fortress walls and open courtyards make it one of the most distinctive night-time venues in Poland. There is genuinely no better backdrop for a party of this scale.

## **Heads Up: Dragon Parade in Town**

Kraków’s annual **Dragon Parade (Parada Smoków)** takes place the same evening. Streets around the Old Town and the Vistula riverbank will be busy and traffic will be rerouted in places — so plan your journey accordingly and leave a little extra time.
