## The Call for Host City Applications Is Open

Have you ever imagined bringing one of the largest WordPress events in the world to your local community?

The call for the WordCamp Europe 2028 host city is now officially open, and WordPress communities across Europe are invited to apply to host the event.

Every year, WordCamp Europe brings together thousands of attendees, contributors, organisers, sponsors, and volunteers from all over the world to celebrate open source, collaboration, learning, and community.

## Why Host WordCamp Europe?

Hosting WordCamp Europe is an opportunity to showcase not only a city, but also the people, creativity, and energy behind its local WordPress community.

It is a chance to:

- Bring the international WordPress community to your city
- Strengthen and grow your local community
- Create lasting connections and experiences
- Collaborate with organisers from across Europe
- Help shape the future of WordCamp Europe

## Who Can Apply?

Communities across Europe with experience organising local WordCamps are encouraged to apply.

The organising team will consider several factors during the selection process, including:

- The strength and activity of the local WordPress community
- Venue and infrastructure suitability
- Accessibility and transport connections
- Accommodation options for attendees
- The overall vision for the event

Communities from countries that have not previously hosted WordCamp Europe are especially encouraged to apply.

## Support Throughout the Journey

Hosting WordCamp Europe is a major undertaking, but local teams are never alone in the process.

The selected host city will work closely with experienced organisers from previous editions and receive guidance and support throughout the planning journey.

## Ready to Bring WordCamp Europe to Your City?

If your community is excited about the idea of hosting WordCamp Europe 2028, now is the time to explore what it takes and start the conversation locally.
