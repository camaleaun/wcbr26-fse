Welcome to the Kim Parsell Memorial Scholarship page for WordCamp Europe 2026. This inspiring initiative celebrates the legacy of Kim Parsell, a beloved member of the WordPress community whose passion for helping others and dedication to inclusivity continue to open doors for contributors worldwide.

Kim Parsell Scholarship applications closed on March 15th, 2026

What the Scholarship Is

The Kim Parsell Memorial Scholarship provides financial support to one active WordPress contributor who identifies as a woman and has never attended WordCamp Europe before. This year, we’re once again proud to help a deserving community member attend WordCamp Europe 2026 by covering the cost of:

  • Your WordCamp Europe 2026 ticket
  • Round-trip airfare
  • Lodging for the duration of the event

This support is made possible by the WordPress Foundation, continuing Kim’s vision of a more inclusive, diverse, and empowered WordPress community.

Who Should Apply

You’re encouraged to apply if you:

  • Are an active contributor to the WordPress project
  • Identify as a woman
  • Have never attended WordCamp Europe before
  • Have a financial need that might otherwise prevent you from attending

Whether you contribute to core, translations, documentation, design, community events, or any other part of the WordPress ecosystem, your experience matters, and this scholarship exists to help you bring it to a global stage.

Key dates

  • Applications close on 15 March 2026
  • All applicants will be contacted by 15 April 2026

Why This Matters

Being part of WordCamp Europe is more than attending sessions. It is about:

  • Connecting with contributors from across the globe
  • Sharing knowledge and building collaborations
  • Growing your skills and confidence in the WordPress community

This scholarship empowers contributors who might otherwise face financial barriers to attend one of the most inspiring events in our ecosystem. By honouring Kim’s spirit, we help ensure the WordPress community continues to thrive with diverse voices and perspectives.

WHO WAS KIM PARSELL?

Kim Parsell was a deeply cherished member of the WordPress open-source community, known for her warmth, generosity, and unwavering support of others.

She was affectionately called #wpmom for the nurturing support she offered, especially to women breaking into tech, and contributed countless volunteer hours, including work on the documentation team and support for major WordPress releases.

Sadly, Kim passed away in 2015, but her spirit of encouragement and service continues to inspire the global WordPress community. In her memory, the Kim Parsell Memorial Scholarship was established to support women who contribute to WordPress with financial assistance to attend flagship WordCamp events, extending her legacy of connection, inclusivity, and empowerment.
