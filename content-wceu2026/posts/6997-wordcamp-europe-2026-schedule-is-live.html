Your WCEU agenda at a glance:

  • 4 June 2026: Contributor Day: a full-day event where the WordPress community comes together to contribute to the WordPress project in many different ways. (Make sure to register separately for this HERE!) 
  • 5–6 June 2026: Workshops, Presentations, and Parties. The presentations cover a wide range of topics and formats, from fast-paced 10-minute lightning sessions to in-depth 30-minute talks. Expect practical insights on WordPress, accessibility, business, marketing, AI, education, and community, with real-world examples you can apply directly to your work.
  • Evening of 6 June: Closing talk by Matt Mullenweg, Co-founder of WordPress and Founder & CEO of Automattic. Expect powerful insights into the current state of WordPress, the innovations shaping its evolution, and what lies ahead for the open web. Followed by the afterparty (all attendees are invited) to celebrate our hard work and new friendships!
