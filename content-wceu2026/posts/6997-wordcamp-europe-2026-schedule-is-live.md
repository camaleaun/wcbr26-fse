The wait is finally over! WordCamp Europe 2026 in Kraków, Poland, is just around the corner! We are thrilled to release the full schedule, packed with engaging workshops and inspiring talks, with excitement building across the global WordPress community.

This year’s gathering is set to be one of our most vibrant and impactful yet. We are preparing to welcome an expected 3,000+ attendees to the lovely city of Kraków between June 4th and 6th for three days of connection and innovation.

Wondering what awaits you in Poland this June? Worry not! Get ready for an unforgettable experience. You are welcome to join the WordPress Community in celebrating open-source technology, innovative creativity, and meaningful connections. Over the course of the event, you’ll have access to 50+ talks and workshops in total.

We are proud of our lineup of speakers from all around the World, spanning 6 continents, contributing to the success of the largest WordPress conference in the World. Attending WordCamp Europe 2026 in Kraków will allow you to easily customise your learning experience, thanks to multiple tracks featuring dozens of sessions and workshops.

## Your WCEU agenda at a glance:

- **4 June 2026:** Contributor Day: a full-day event where the WordPress community comes together to contribute to the WordPress project in many different ways. *(*[*Make sure to register separately for this HERE!*](https://europe.wordcamp.org/2026/community/contributor-day/)*) *
- **5–6 June 2026:** Workshops, Presentations, and Parties. The presentations cover a wide range of topics and formats, from fast-paced 10-minute lightning sessions to in-depth 30-minute talks. Expect practical insights on WordPress, accessibility, business, marketing, AI, education, and community, with real-world examples you can apply directly to your work.
- **Evening of 6 June:** Closing talk by Matt Mullenweg, Co-founder of WordPress and Founder & CEO of Automattic. Expect powerful insights into the current state of WordPress, the innovations shaping its evolution, and what lies ahead for the open web. Followed by <span style="margin: 0px;padding: 0px">the** afterparty**</span> (all attendees are invited) to celebrate our hard work and new friendships!

### Why you shouldn’t miss out on Kraków 2026?

WordCamp Europe is more than just a conference; it is an annual family reunion for the people who build, design, and market the modern web.

This year, the sessions are highly tailored to the most pressing trends in our industry. You will walk away from our hands-on workshops with actionable frameworks you can implement in the next week. Furthermore, the spontaneous conversations you will have between sessions while grabbing a coffee or exploring our sponsor hall often lead to the most valuable insights and lifelong friendships.

Kraków itself provides the perfect backdrop. As a city steeped in history yet buzzing with modern tech innovation, it mirrors the very essence of WordPress: honouring our foundational roots while constantly evolving for the future.
