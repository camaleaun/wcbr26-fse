If you’ve never done a workshop before, think of it as the thinking person’s track session. Instead of sitting back and listening, you’re actually **learning by building, experimenting, and asking questions in real time**. It’s the difference between watching someone cook and getting your hands in the kitchen yourself.

You will learn how to build and submit WordPress plugins to the directory, learn more about different useful API practices, get insights from the worlds of WooCommerce and business development, and the list goes on.

## What to know before you register?

**Spaces Fill Up**: Seats are limited to keep workshops manageable and engaging. Once a workshop is full, registration closes. But don’t worry, there’s a queuing system outside the workshop room on the day, and if someone doesn’t show up, you get in. Fair and square.

**Some Workshops Have Tech Requirements**: Most are hands-on, so you might need a laptop, specific software installed, or an account set up beforehand. We’ve listed exactly what each workshop needs, so you can check whether it’s a good fit for your setup.

**Show Up Early**: If you pre-registered, arrive at least 10 minutes early. We want to make sure you get a seat and have time to settle in before things kick off.

**You Can Still Cancel**: Plans change, and that’s okay. If you need to cancel your registration, just [contact our Speakers Team](https://europe.wordcamp.org/2026/contact/contact-our-speakers-team/).
