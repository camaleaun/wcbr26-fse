Accommodation in and around Kraków

Kraków offers a wide range of accommodation options to suit every budget and travel style. From boutique hotels in the historic Old Town and stylish stays in Kazimierz to budget-friendly hostels and modern flats, the city has no shortage of places to rest after a full day of talks and networking.

The compact city layout and excellent public transport make it easy to stay slightly outside the city centre without sacrificing convenience. Many areas are well-connected by tram and bus, allowing you to reach the venue quickly while enjoying quieter neighbourhoods and often better prices.

Alternatives To Hotels

Hostels

Kraków is known for its high-quality hostels, ranging from social, budget-friendly options to quieter, private-room setups. They are a good choice if you want affordability and a chance to meet fellow attendees.

Guesthouses and B&BS

Smaller guesthouses and B&Bs offer a more personal experience, often run by locals and located in charming neighbourhoods just outside the main tourist areas. They are ideal if you prefer a quieter stay with local character.

Student Residencies

Outside the academic term, some student residences and budget accommodations open their rooms to visitors, offering simple and affordable stays with good public transport links to the city centre.
