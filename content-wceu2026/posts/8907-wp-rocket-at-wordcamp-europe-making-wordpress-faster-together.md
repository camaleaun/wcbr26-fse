Today, more than 5 million websites worldwide rely on WP Rocket. From freelancers and  agencies to ecommerce businesses and site owners, our users count on us to improve  loading times, enhance PageSpeed score, and optimize Core Web Vitals with minimal  configuration. 

WP Rocket enables WordPress professionals to save time while delivering measurable  performance gains. And performance is not only about improving a score: it affects user  experience, search rankings, and conversions, ultimately supporting real business growth.
