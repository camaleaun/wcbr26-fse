## Top 5 reasons your agency should attend WordCamp Europe 2026

Experience three days of talks, workshops, and connections designed for every role in the ecosystem.

WordCamp Europe is the continent’s largest gathering of WordPress professionals, bringing together developers, designers, and agency owners to share knowledge, build connections, and shape the future of the platform that powers over 40% of the web. This three-day event combines inspiring talks, hands-on workshops, and unparalleled networking opportunities that directly impact your agency’s growth and expertise.
