Like we have every year, the Community Booth is available to attendees of WCEU 2026 in Kraków!

It is in a central area, right in the heart of the venue, dedicated to the WordPress Community and its activities!

Experienced members of the Community will be there to welcome and help you find information on almost anything that has to do with WordPress, i.e. technical issues, how to contribute back to the project, what is “hot” this year, etc.

Want to discuss a related topic and never had the chance to do so in the past? This is the perfect opportunity for you!

Meet up with friends, colleagues, and even make some new connections over at the WordPress Community Booth!

Ask us anything

If you have questions, we’ve got the answers! Unsure about how to get the most out of your attendee experience? Lost? Questions about food, sessions, sponsors, WordPress, and contributing to WordPress? Questions about the very meaning of life? Need help with an issue or concern? 

Staffed with members of the WordPress community who are passionate about helping you find the answers and resources you need, we’ll be there for you! Stop by, say hello, and stay a while to keep us company! We’ll have computers and a large screen to walk you through relevant information.

Get involved in the community

Have you ever wanted to up your WordPress game and give a little back to the community, but aren’t sure how to do that? Did you know there are more than a dozen ways to share your expertise with the WordPress project that don’t involve writing code? 

Speak with members of various Making WordPress teams about volunteer opportunities, the Five for the Future program, and how you can help shape the future of WordPress. We’ll show you which teams exist within the WordPress community and how to get involved.

Want to find out even more about contributing?

No matter what your field or expertise level is, there’s always room to contribute!

Explore our activities

In addition to all the community activities, we offer you more unforgettable experiences.

Make your participation unforgettable and pose in front of our photo wall. A variety of backdrops are available.

Show where you traveled from and add your personal pin to a global, digital map.

Test your knowledge and take a quiz. If you can’t answer all the questions, you’re sure to learn something new.

Do you like tricky tasks? Try our WordCamp Europe 2026 puzzle and show how quickly you can put it together.

Small puzzle pieces with a cool design make puzzling together unforgettable. Be a part of it and complete a puzzle together with others.

Our highlight is the Claw Machine. Here you can get your own personal dragon in the WordCamp Europe 2026 design.
