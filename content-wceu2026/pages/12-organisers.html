We’d like to introduce you to the WordCamp Europe 2026 Organising Team!

Behind the scenes, you’ll find a variety of volunteer organisers from across the continent and beyond, helping to plan and prepare for the largest WordCamp event in Europe!

All of our organisers dedicate their time and skills to help bring our community together for an amazing few days to learn, collaborate and celebrate WordPress Community.

We have three dedicated and experienced Lead Organisers who will be overseeing the organisation of our event and supporting our team members.

They ensure everything runs smoothly by adding their unique flair and previous WordCamp insights to create the atmosphere and good energy for WordCamp Europe 2026.
