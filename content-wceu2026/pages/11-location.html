Welcome to the Heart of Poland

WordCamp Europe 2026 is set in vibrant Kraków, a city where history meets creativity. Wander through charming old town streets. Taste world-class food and coffee. Explore rich culture, iconic architecture, and warm hospitality. Kraków invites you to learn, explore, and make unforgettable memories.

Get to know Kraków

Experience the futuristic conference venue

ICE Kraków Congress Centre, located in the heart of the city, welcomes WordCamp Europe 2026 attendees to its state-of-the-art halls and stunning panoramic foyer. With world-class infrastructure, exceptional accessibility, and inspiring views of Wawel Castle and the Vistula River, it offers the perfect setting for our community to learn, connect, and create together.
