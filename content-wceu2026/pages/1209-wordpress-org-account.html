A WordPress.org account is required to purchase tickets.

Verifying your WordPress.org account can take a few hours to a couple of days. To ensure a smooth registration process and avoid delays, please create an account or have your login credentials ready before purchasing a ticket.

If you’re unfamiliar with the registration process, we recommend referring to the tutorial on Learn WordPress. This tutorial walks you through each step to help you complete the registration process quickly. It covers everything from filling out your account information to verifying your email, making it helpful for first-time users of WordPress.org.

If you encounter any issues during registration or need assistance with ticket management, please feel free to contact the WordCamp Europe team, and we are always here to help.
