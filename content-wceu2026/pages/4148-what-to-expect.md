### 4th June 2026: Contributor Day

**Contributor Day is a full-day event where the WordPress community comes together to contribute to the WordPress project in many different ways. Whether you are new to contributing or have years of experience, everyone is welcome and supported throughout the day. \
\
Entry to Contributor Day is included in your WordCamp Europe ticket, but a separate registration will be required closer to the event.**

### 5th–6th June 2026: Workshops, Presentations, and Parties

The presentations at WordCamp Europe cover a wide range of topics and formats, from fast-paced 10-minute lightning sessions to in-depth 30-minute talks. Expect practical insights on WordPress, accessibility, business, marketing, AI, education, and community, with real-world examples you can apply directly to your work.

### Parties

Our parties are a chance to relax and connect after a full day of learning. **All attendees are invited to the official WordCamp Europe After Party** on the evening of 6th June. In addition, some sponsors host evening side events throughout the conference. They are organised separately and usually require their own registration.
