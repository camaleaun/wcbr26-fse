## How to Join Side Events

Please note that **availability and registration are managed by the individual organisers**. Be sure to read the instructions carefully to find out how to sign up or confirm your attendance.

Check out the schedule below and make the most of your time at WordCamp Europe!

***Note**: Side events are not official WordCamp Europe events but can be organised by anyone attending the conference who would like to set up a get-together. The organisers of these events agree to adhere to the Code of Conduct, and WordCamp Europe Organisers are not involved in organising these events. For special requests such as accessibility needs, dietary requirements, etc., please reach out to the event organiser directly.*
