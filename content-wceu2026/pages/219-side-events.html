Looking to add something extra to your WordCamp Europe experience? Our line-up of side events will do that.

We will assemble a vibrant mix of activities designed to complement the leading conference, whether you’re hoping to expand your network, explore the city, or have fun with fellow attendees.

There will be something for everyone — from casual meetups to unique experiences that bring the WordPress community even closer.

How to Join Side Events

Please note that availability and registration are managed by the individual organisers. Be sure to read the instructions carefully to find out how to sign up or confirm your attendance.

Check out the schedule below and make the most of your time at WordCamp Europe!

Note: Side events are not official WordCamp Europe events but can be organised by anyone attending the conference who would like to set up a get-together. The organisers of these events agree to adhere to the Code of Conduct, and WordCamp Europe Organisers are not involved in organising these events. For special requests such as accessibility needs, dietary requirements, etc., please reach out to the event organiser directly.

More side events will be announced soon!

Make Your Mark at WCEU. Submit your side event!

Have an idea that could bring people together during WCEU? This is your chance to make it happen.

We’re inviting organisers, communities, and companies to host side events that add energy, insight, and connection to the WCEU experience. Whether it’s a workshop, networking gathering, panel discussion, social meetup, or something entirely different, we’d love to hear what you have in mind.

If you’re interested in organising an event during WCEU, complete the form below to tell us about your proposal.

All submissions will be carefully reviewed by our team. Approved side events will be featured and shared with attendees, giving you visibility and helping participants discover even more ways to connect.

Apply by May 24, 2026

To be considered, your event should

  • Be open and accessible to all WCEU participants
  • Encourage community, collaboration, and meaningful connections
  • Comply fully with the WCEU Code of Conduct
