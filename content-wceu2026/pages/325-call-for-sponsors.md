### ****Become part of the story: sponsor WordCamp Europe 2026****

Every WordCamp Europe captures the spirit of its host city, and 2026 will be no exception.

In Kraków (<a href="https://icekrakow.pl/en/about-ice-krakow" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Krakow Congress Center</a>), where centuries of history meet a thriving digital scene, the global WordPress community will gather to share ideas, build connections, and shape the future of the open web.

By sponsoring WordCamp Europe 2026, you’re not just supporting an event; you’re joining a community movement that celebrates collaboration, innovation, and open-source values.

Your brand will be visible to thousands of professionals: developers, designers, agencies, product builders, educators, and creators who make WordPress what it is today.

This is your opportunity to connect directly with an engaged, international audience and to help make one of the world’s largest WordPress events possible.

### **Why sponsor WordCamp Europe 2026?**

Building on the long-running success of past WCEU events across Europe, we’re ready to make Kraków our most inspiring and impactful edition yet.

Sponsoring WCEU means:

- Visibility: Your brand showcased to a diverse global audience.
- Engagement: Real conversations with the people who design, develop, and power WordPress.
- Networking: Access to potential partners, clients, and top talent in the ecosystem.
- Contribution: Giving back to the open-source project that drives millions of websites around the world.

Some indicative numbers from the 2025 edition:

- 1,700+ attendees
- 80+ countries represented
- 600+ contributors
- 20+ make teams
- 30+ table leads
- 50+ speakers

Sponsorship is more than brand exposure. It’s a chance to become part of something bigger, to connect with the community that keeps the web open and accessible.
