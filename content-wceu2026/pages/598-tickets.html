This is where you can purchase tickets for WordCamp Europe 2026, which will be held in Kraków, Poland, from 4 to 6 June 2026.

Contributor Day will take place on 4 June 2026, followed by the WordCamp Europe conference on 5 and 6 June 2026.

Tickets include entry to our two-day event, lunch, coffee, snacks, the opportunity to register for Contributor Day, and an invite to the After Party on 6th June. You’ll also connect with others from the community.

It’s important to us that everyone who attends WordCamp Europe is treated equally, respected, and protected. By attending, you agree to abide by our Code of Conduct.

Ticket options

There are two ticket options to choose from.

General ticket

Our General Ticket is priced far below the actual cost of attendance because we’re dedicated to keeping WCEU affordable.

Sponsors help cover the cost of an event that passionately focuses on delivering the best experience, subsidising and offsetting the low price of General Tickets.

Micro-Sponsor ticket

These tickets are priced closer to the actual cost of attendance. If you can donate a little extra, please consider purchasing a Micro-Sponsor ticket to give back. Your donation ensures an extra-special WCEU experience for you and all other attendees.

As a small thank you, Micro-sponsors will be featured on a dedicated page on the WCEU website, with their name and a link of their choice, recognising their extra support for the community.

Important notes

  • A WordPress.org profile is required to buy a ticket.
  • Each ticket is valid for one person only. If you are bringing a guest, they’ll need their own ticket.
  • When purchasing more than one ticket, each ticket must be assigned to a specific person, using their full name and an individual email address that is connected to a WordPress.org account. Only the person assigned to a ticket can edit the ticket details or transfer the ticket to someone else. Refer to this image as a reference on how ticket editing looks like. If you do not know an attendee’s email address at the time of purchase, you may use unknown.attendee@example.org as email address and update the details later. The buyer will need to make this change.
  • Tickets are refundable. Please see our Refund Information page for further details. Tickets are only refundable until 18th May 2026.
  • When requesting a refund, all tickets purchased together will be cancelled. We are unable to provide partial refunds or transfers to a different account.
  • You can sell, give, or gift a ticket you bought by editing the attendee details using the link in your ticket purchase confirmation email. You can also view our Privacy Policy.
  • The requests for visa invitation letters closed on 10 May 2026
  • If you have opted in to be listed on the Attendees page, the following information will be included. You can hide this information by following the link in your confirmation email.
    • Name on the Attendees Page.
    • Profile picture from Gravatar.com.
  • For community-building and promotional purposes, there will be volunteer and/or professional photographers and videographers at the WordCamp event. By attending the event, you consent to your image being used in resulting photos or videos that may show your participation in the WordCamp.
