## **Excited for WordCamp Europe 2026? Let the world know!**

Join thousands of WordPress enthusiasts at the **biggest WordPress event in the world!!**

This year, we designed colourful badge frames for you to customise with your own profile picture and brand!

Show your excitement by using the badges below on your website, blog, or social media. Don’t forget to tag us **@WCEurope** and use **\#WCEU** to connect with the community! You can also link back to <a href="http://europe.wordcamp.org/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">europe.wordcamp.org</a> to spread the word.

### Make your own badge

Click on the badge, upload your photo, and you are ready to go!
