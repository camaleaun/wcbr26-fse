Parking

Parking is available at the Phoenix Convention Center in the North Garage at a rate of around $21.50 per day. You may reserve parking through ParkPHX here. Note this is a cashless venue and only accepts credit/debit payments. For more information, you can visit the parking page directly on the PCC’s website. 

Getting Here

For directions and instructions on arriving at the PCC via lightrail, public transit, rideshares, scooters, and car, please visit the transportation page directly on the PCC’s website. 

Accessibility 

The Phoenix Convention Center is an Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) compliant facility, and prides itself in creating an inclusive space for all to enjoy. You can learn more about the venue’s accessibility efforts by visiting the accessibility information page.
