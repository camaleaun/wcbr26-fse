## What is WordCamp US? 

WordCamp is a community-organized conference dedicated to WordPress, an open-source platform that powers 40% of the web. It’s a place for WordPress enthusiasts, users, developers, designers, and other participants to network, share ideas, and learn about WordPress.

## Who typically attends WordCamp US (audience demographics, roles, industries)?

WordCamps attract a diverse audience from across the WordPress community, making them vibrant and inclusive events. Attendees typically include WordPress users of all skill levels, from complete beginners to experienced developers and designers. The events also draw agencies and freelancers offering WordPress-related services, along with community organizers, volunteers, and contributors to the WordPress open-source project. WordCamp US, in particular, brings together people from around the world, fostering a collaborative atmosphere where professionals, enthusiasts, and newcomers can network, learn, and share their expertise.
