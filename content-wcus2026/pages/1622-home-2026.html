WordCamp US brings together the community of WordPress users, developers, and contributors for four days of learning, building, and connecting. Your ticket includes Contributor Day, Showcase Day, and two conference days, as well as lunch, snacks, and a community social.

Meet Fellow Builders

See real projects in motion on ShowCase Day and then attend any of 50+ talks and workshops. Walk the Sponsor Hall and strike up conversations with peers, speakers, and businesses.

Make New Connections

Looking for connections? Head to the Career Corner to browse the job board, see who’s hiring, and meet with company reps.

Get Help and Get Started

The Happiness Bar is a free, drop-in help desk where you can sit down with a volunteer WordPress expert and work through whatever has you stuck.

Conveniently located at the Phoenix Convention Center in the heart of Arizona’s beautiful capital city, guests arriving through Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport (PHX) are less than 5 miles away. Book your stay at the Sheraton Phoenix Downtown for easy access and special rates.

Bringing the family or staying a few extra days? Spend time in the profoundly spiritual landscape of the Sonoran Desert or visit the Roosevelt Row arts district’s galleries and independent restaurants. Nearby Chase Field and the Footprint Center host professional baseball and live shows, and the surrounding desert offers striking scenery at places like Papago Park and the Desert Botanical Garden.

Build. Publish. Belong.

WordPress empowers people to build, publish, and grow online with the freedom to turn ideas into thriving businesses and communities.

Inspiration, passion, and community await! For over a decade, WordCamps have welcomed longtime users and beginners looking to plug into the power of the open web for the first time. There’s something for everyone at WordCamp US.
