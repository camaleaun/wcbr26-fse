Join us August 16–19 at the Phoenix Convention Center

Every WordCamp US ticket covers the full event: sessions led by the people running some of the largest WordPress sites on the web, hands-on workshops where you build something to take back to your own projects, and free one-on-one help at the Happiness Bar. Attendees leave with new skills, new contributor accounts, and job leads from the Career Corner.

Please view this page in a browser to purchase or manage tickets.

This year’s program offers four tracks covering everything from AI to WordPress essentials for beginners. The AI track gets practical, with sessions covering AI-assisted development, preparing your site for AI agents, and weighing the legal and ethical uncertainties. On the technical side, talks cover modern development workflows and automation, while business sessions tackle pricing, maintenance, and how the agency model is evolving. Newcomers get their own track of approachable sessions built just for them.

Health & Safety 

We invite you to help us make WordCamps a welcome and safe experience for everyone. When planning to attend WordCamp, we recommend that you stay at home if you are sick or have recently come in contact with someone who is ill.

If you see another attendee wearing a sticker requesting that people wear a mask near them, please wear a mask within 6 feet (2 meters) of them or keep your distance.

Dietary Restrictions

We want everyone at WordCamp US to feel welcome—and well-fed. Each day, we offer lunch options that support a variety of dietary needs. If you don’t see your specific restriction listed, just let us know in the “Meal preference details” section of your registration, and we’ll do our best to accommodate you.

Severe Allergies

Your health and safety matter to us. If you indicate a severe allergy on your ticket form via the radio icon—severe meaning one that requires immediate medical attention if triggered by contact, ingestion, or inhalation—a member of our team will reach out to follow up and make sure we can help create a safe environment for you.

GDPR & Data Privacy

We collect personal and demographic information to help us plan an inclusive, community-focused event and ensure a great experience for all attendees. Your information will never be sold, and we only share details when necessary for essential event operations (like badge printing or dietary accommodations).

Demographic responses are optional, used in aggregate to better understand our audience and inform future WordCamp planning.

All data is stored securely and handled in accordance with applicable privacy laws. 

For more information visit wordpress.org/about/privacy/.
