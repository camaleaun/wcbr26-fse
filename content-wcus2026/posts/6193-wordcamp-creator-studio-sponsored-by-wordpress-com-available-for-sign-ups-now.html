WordCamp US is offering a bookable recording studio in Phoenix, sponsored by WordPress.com. We welcome creators, influencers, podcasters, and anyone interested in recording content to take advantage of this customizable on-site recording space by reserving a session through our booking system. The Creator Studio is available August 16-19, from 9:00am-4:00pm. 

The Purpose

WordPress is powered by the people – this space is designed to give attendees the tools and opportunity to share their voices and shape how the world experiences WordCamp. It’s part content creation, part connection, and all community.

Use the space to:

  • Host a short podcast interview with a speaker or attendee. 
  • Record your reflections, insights, or favorite sessions. 
  • Collaborate with fellow attendees on a quick piece of creative content on your channels. 

We want to help amplify the voices of the people who power WordPress — the makers, thinkers, and storytellers who bring it to life every day!

How It Works

  • Complete this intake form to indicate your interest. We have limited availability / all slots are on a first-come, first-served basis. Only registered attendees of WordCamp US will be able to book the Creator Studio. Please make sure to secure your event pass if you haven’t already done so. 
  • Available slots are August 16–19 (Sunday–Wednesday), from 9:00am–4:00pm.
  • This room will be closed to all other attendees during your recording time. 
  • Sessions are 60 minutes maximum, with 30 minutes of setup time beforehand.
  • Only one session per creator/group to ensure fairness.
  • A volunteer and an audio-visual tech will be present to manage the room and keep time.
  • We provide basic audio-visual equipment; you’re also welcome to bring your own.
    • We will have the following equipment available for your use. 

Video

  • (1) Sony HXC-100 Triax HD Camera – Pro-level HD video camera with crisp resolution
  • (1) Tripod – Stable, smooth support for clean camera shots.
  • BMD Recording – Captures your recording to a portable drive.

Audio

  • Shure UDR Wireless Mic System – Broadcast-quality wireless microphones.
  • Boom Mic – Ideal for clean voice capture
  • Audio Mixer – For managing mic levels and audio inputs.
  • Boom Pole + Mount – Flexible mic positioning for interviews or solo recording.

Lighting 

  • FloLight FL-330AW 330W – For soft, even lighting on faces.
  • Photography Light Package – Adjustable color and brightness.

Backdrop

  • (1) Branded Backdrop (10’x10’ – A step & repeat style branded WordCamp backdrop).
