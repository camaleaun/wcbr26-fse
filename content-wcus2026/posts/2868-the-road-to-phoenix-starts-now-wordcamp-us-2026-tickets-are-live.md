Route 66 runs directly through Arizona and perfectly grounds this year’s experience in the vibrant landscape of the Southwest. It’s no coincidence that this historic highway symbolizes connection and shared journeys — much like the spirit that unites the WordPress community.

The pathways the WordPress community has created for collaboration and creativity are just as inspiring as the destination. With a playful nod to the year itself, our WordCamp US 2026 theme, Route 66, reflects a simple, powerful idea: *we are all traveling the same road together, building something greater through connection.\
\*
But a great road trip is nothing without the people who share the ride. This year in Phoenix, we want you on the map with us! Whether you are looking to contribute, attend, volunteer, speak, or sponsor, your journey starts today.

## Join Us as a Guest

Ready to experience the best of WordPress? As a guest, you’ll have front-row access to groundbreaking sessions, invaluable networking opportunities, and the incredible energy of the global WordPress community. Whether you’re a developer, designer, content creator, business owner, or student, this is your space to learn and grow.

## Join Us as a Volunteer

WordCamp US is built by the community, for the community. By joining our volunteer team, you get a behind-the-scenes look at how a massive production comes together. It’s an incredible opportunity to give back, make lifelong friends, and be the driving force behind WordCamp US 2026’s success. We look forward to welcoming you on the team with a free general admission ticket on us.
