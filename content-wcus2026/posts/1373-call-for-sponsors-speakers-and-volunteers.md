## Call for Sponsors

Sponsorships are what make WordCamp US possible.

Your support enables an accessible, high-quality experience for thousands of attendees from around the world. Sponsorships help maintain low ticket prices while investing in the content, production, and community that define a flagship WordCamp.

In return, sponsors gain meaningful visibility within one of the most engaged ecosystems in tech.

**Why sponsor WordCamp US:**

- Connect directly with a global audience of WordPress users, builders, and decision-makers.
- Showcase your brand through high-impact on-site experiences and activations.
- Gain visibility across the website, livestream, and attendee communications.
- Build relationships with agencies, developers, and enterprise customers.
- Align your brand with the mission and future of open source.

A range of sponsorship opportunities are available, with options for both in-person and digital participation.

## Call for Speakers

Have something to share with the WordPress community?

Speaker applications are now open for WordCamp US 2026. We’re looking for voices across the community – developers, designers, marketers, creators, and beyond.

You don’t need to be a seasoned speaker. If you have a strong idea and something valuable to share, we want to hear from you.

**We’re looking for:**

- Real-world insights and practical takeaways.
- Personal stories and lessons learned.
- Content that educates, inspires, or challenges thinking.
- New and underrepresented perspectives.
- Ideas shaping what’s next (including AI, publishing, and the open web).

Formats range from traditional talks to workshops and more interactive sessions.

📅 *Speaker application deadline: May 29, 2026*

## Call for Volunteers

Volunteers are the heartbeat of WordCamp US. From supporting sessions to welcoming attendees, volunteers help create the experience that makes WordCamp special.

Whether it’s your first time or you’re a long-time contributor, there’s a place for you.

**Why volunteer:**

- Be part of the team behind a flagship WordPress event.
- Meet and collaborate with people from around the world.
- Gain behind-the-scenes experience.
- Help create an inclusive, welcoming environment.
- Receive a free ticket to WordCamp US.

No prior experience is required, just a willingness to get involved.

📅 *Volunteer application deadline: June 15, 2026*
