The WordPress Foundation is pleased to announce the return of the Kim Parsell Memorial Scholarship for WordCamp US 2026. Applications are open now and will be accepted through **July 10, 2026**.

## **Remembering Kim Parsell**

Kim Parsell was a dedicated contributor and a beloved member of the WordPress community. Her passion for open source and her welcoming spirit inspired many, both online and in person. Each year at WordCamp US, the WordPress Foundation celebrates Kim’s legacy by supporting contributors who share her commitment and enthusiasm. The Kim Parsell Memorial Scholarship exists to make it easier for deserving community members to attend and make a difference. This reflects Kim’s belief that WordPress should be accessible and inclusive for everyone, at every step of their journey.

If you’re unfamiliar with Kim’s story or her invaluable role in the community, we encourage you to [read these heartfelt tributes](https://us.wordcamp.org/2019/memories-of-kim-parsell/) collected from friends and colleagues who knew her best.

## **Scholarship Eligibility**

This year, one scholarship will be awarded. To qualify, applicants must:

- Identify as a woman.
- Be actively involved as a contributor to WordPress.
- Have never attended WordCamp US before.
- Demonstrate a financial need to attend the event.

If you meet these qualifications, we invite you to apply before the **July 10 deadline**. All applicants will be notified of the decision by **July 24, 2026**.

The WordPress Foundation will cover the cost of the event ticket, round-trip flight, and lodging. We can’t wait to welcome you to Phoenix! 

For more information, visit the [Kim Parsell Memorial Scholarship page](https://wordpressfoundation.org/projects/kim-parsell-memorial-scholarship/) on the WordPress Foundation website.
